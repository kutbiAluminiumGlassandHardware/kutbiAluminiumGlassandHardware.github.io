Sliding window repair services

When an aluminium sliding window becomes difficult to open, noisy or impossible to lock, the cause is often worn hardware rather than the whole frame. Kutbi checks the window and recommends a practical repair.

  • Aluminium sliding window repair
  • Window roller replacement
  • Window wheel replacement
  • Window lock and latch replacement
  • Window handle replacement
  • Rubber beading replacement
  • Track and alignment checks
  • Aluminium sliding door hardware repair

Repairing existing aluminium windows

A usable frame can often be retained by replacing worn rollers, wheels, locks, handles or seals. We assess the frame, glass, track and hardware before suggesting repair or replacement. This helps avoid unnecessary replacement when a component-level repair is practical.
