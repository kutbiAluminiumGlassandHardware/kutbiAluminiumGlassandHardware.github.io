Common causes

Understanding the actual cause helps avoid unnecessary replacement. Kutbi checks the relevant rollers, wheels, tracks, alignment, handles, locks and other compatible hardware before recommending the suitable repair.

1. Worn or damaged rollers and wheels

Flattened, cracked or worn wheels may cause the sash to drag, sit unevenly or make a grinding sound. Roller type and size vary by window system, so compatibility should be checked before a replacement is selected.

2. Track wear, obstruction or alignment problems

Dirt or debris may interrupt movement, while a worn or damaged track can keep a sound roller from running smoothly. The track and wheel should be inspected together rather than assuming the wheel alone is at fault.

3. Loose, broken or faulty handles and locks

If the sash slides but will not close or lock, the issue may be in the handle, locking points or alignment rather than the rollers. Check the symptom separately from movement resistance.

4. Window alignment or uneven sash

An uneven sash or inconsistent gap can indicate an alignment issue. Replacing rollers without checking the sash and frame may not resolve the underlying cause.

5. When replacement may be needed

If a compatible roller is cracked, badly worn or no longer turns properly, replacement may be more suitable than adjustment. The existing system should be inspected first to identify the correct component.

What to Tell the Repair Technician

  • Whether the window is noisy, dragging, uneven or completely stuck.
  • Whether the problem occurs throughout the track or at one point.
  • Whether the sash also fails to close or lock.
  • Whether a wheel looks cracked, loose or visibly worn.

These details help narrow the inspection, but the roller and track should be checked in person before deciding on a repair.
