Compatible Aluminium Window Beading

Different aluminium window systems can use different profiles. Kutbi checks the existing section and glazing setup rather than assuming one profile will fit every window.

Rubber Beading Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the existing rubber beading and glazing.
  2. Check the aluminium frame and affected section.
  3. Identify the suitable compatible beading profile.
  4. Remove deteriorated material where required.
  5. Install the suitable replacement carefully around the glazing.
  6. Check the finished fit and surrounding frame.

Warning Signs Your Beading Needs Attention

Rubber has become hard

Hardened or brittle material may no longer provide the same fit.

Beading is coming loose

Loose sections should be inspected before they deteriorate further.

Visible gaps

Gaps around glazing can indicate a sealing or beading issue that needs assessment.

Damaged or missing sections

Cracked, torn or missing beading may require compatible replacement.

Repair or Beading Replacement?

Depending on the condition, a small issue may be addressed without changing unrelated window components. When the rubber itself is worn, damaged or unsuitable, compatible replacement is the appropriate service.

Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and checks the existing system before recommending additional work.

FAQs

What is aluminium window rubber beading?

It is the compatible rubber or sealing profile used around glazing in suitable aluminium window systems.

When should aluminium window beading be replaced?

Replacement may be needed when the existing beading is hard, loose, damaged, missing or no longer fits correctly.

Can old beading cause gaps around glass?

Worn or damaged beading can contribute to gaps, so the glazing and frame should be inspected together.

Does beading replacement require a new window?

No. Beading can often be replaced while retaining a suitable existing aluminium window system.

Need Aluminium Window Rubber Beading Replacement?
