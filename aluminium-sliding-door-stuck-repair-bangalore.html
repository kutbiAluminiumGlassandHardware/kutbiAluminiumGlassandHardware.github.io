Why an Aluminium Sliding Door Gets Stuck

Worn wheels or rollers

Damaged, seized or worn wheels can make the door heavy and eventually stop movement.

Door Roller Repair →

Track problem

Obstruction, wear or damage in the accessible track can interfere with movement.

Alignment problem

An uneven or misaligned door panel can rub and become difficult to slide.

Handle or lock issue

Hardware can sometimes prevent the door from releasing or operating correctly.

Repair-First Diagnosis

  1. Inspect the existing aluminium sliding door and understand when it became stuck.
  2. Check wheels, rollers, track, panel alignment and relevant hardware.
  3. Identify the component or condition causing the movement problem.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible component replacement where practical.
  5. Check sliding, closing and locking after the work.
