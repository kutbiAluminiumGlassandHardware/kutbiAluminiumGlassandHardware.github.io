Common Problems We Check

Door rubbing or dragging

Misalignment can make a uPVC door rub against the frame or floor and become difficult to operate.

Door not closing

Panel or hardware position can prevent the door from reaching its correct closing position.

Lock not lining up

Alignment can affect whether the locking points meet correctly.

Sliding door alignment

For suitable uPVC sliding doors, rollers, tracks and panel position are checked together.

Handle and hardware strain

Poor alignment can put extra load on handles, locks and related hardware.

Sealing gaps

A misaligned door can create uneven gaps that affect sealing and closing.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the existing system before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement. We look at the related parts that can cause the same symptom instead of assuming one component is always responsible.

  1. Inspect the reported problem and operating condition.
  2. Check the related hardware, alignment, sealing or sliding mechanism.
  3. Identify the likely cause from the existing system.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement where appropriate.
  5. Test operation, closing and locking after the work.
