uPVC Window Hardware Problems We Inspect

We assess faulty handles, locks, rollers and related hardware and recommend suitable adjustment, repair or compatible replacement for the existing uPVC window.

uPVC Window Handles

Loose, broken, stiff or difficult-to-operate handles can affect opening and closing. We check the handle, fixing points and operating mechanism before recommending a suitable repair or compatible replacement.

uPVC Window Locks

If a window is not locking, not latching or feels difficult to lock or unlock, we inspect the handle-lock relationship, alignment and fixing points.

Rollers & Wheels

For sliding uPVC windows, worn or damaged rollers can cause hard movement, noise or uneven travel. We check the rollers together with the track and alignment.

Alignment & Operation

Hardware problems can be linked with sash alignment, track condition or frame movement. We diagnose the complete operating system instead of replacing parts unnecessarily.

Compatible uPVC Window Hardware

uPVC window systems can use different hardware profiles and operating components. Kutbi checks the existing window, dimensions, fixing pattern and mechanism before selecting a suitable compatible part where replacement is required.

  • Handle and locking mechanism check
  • Roller or wheel inspection for sliding windows
  • Fixing points and screws
  • Track, frame and sash condition
  • Compatibility before replacement
Diagnosis first

How We Diagnose uPVC Window Hardware Problems

We first identify the operating symptom and inspect the related hardware. A stiff handle may be connected to alignment, while a sliding window that is hard to move may need roller or track attention. This helps keep the repair focused on the actual cause.

  1. Understand the window problem and operating symptom.
  2. Inspect the relevant handle, lock, roller and fixing points.
  3. Check alignment, track and frame condition where applicable.
  4. Recommend adjustment, repair or compatible replacement based on the condition.
Repair process

uPVC Window Hardware Repair Process

Our repair-first process is designed to restore normal operation while avoiding unnecessary replacement of usable components.

1. Inspect

Check the hardware and the window system around it.

2. Identify

Separate a hardware fault from alignment, track or frame-related problems.

3. Repair

Adjust or repair suitable components where practical.

4. Replace if Needed

Use a compatible replacement when the existing component is damaged or no longer serviceable.

FAQs

uPVC Window Hardware Repair FAQs

Can a uPVC window handle be repaired?

Sometimes. The handle, fixing points and operating mechanism are inspected first. If the handle is damaged beyond repair, a compatible replacement may be recommended.

Why is my sliding uPVC window hard to move?

Possible causes include worn rollers, track problems, alignment issues or frame-related movement. The complete operating system should be checked before choosing a part.

Do you replace uPVC window hardware?

Yes, suitable compatible hardware can be replaced when the existing component is damaged or no longer serviceable.

Do you repair both windows and doors?

Yes. Kutbi handles suitable aluminium and uPVC window and door repair, including hardware-related problems.
