uPVC Sliding Window Stuck in Bangalore

A stuck uPVC sliding window may need roller, track, alignment, handle or lock attention rather than complete replacement.

Repair-first service: Kutbi repairs existing uPVC sliding windows and checks the movement, alignment and locking after the repair.

Common Causes of a Stuck uPVC Sliding Window

← Back to uPVC Window Repair · uPVC Sliding Window Repair · uPVC Sliding Window Hard to Slide

  • Worn or damaged rollers and wheels
  • Track wear, obstruction or alignment problems
  • Loose, broken or faulty handles and locks
  • Window or door alignment issues
  • Damaged or worn hardware

How Kutbi handles the problem

  1. Send a photo of the window, door or hardware on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us what is stuck, difficult, broken or not working properly.
  3. We inspect the relevant rollers, wheels, tracks, handles, locks and alignment.
  4. We repair the problem or replace suitable compatible parts.
  5. We check the window or door again for smoother operation.
