What Can Make a uPVC Sliding Window Stuck?

A stuck or hard-to-slide uPVC window can be caused by worn rollers or wheels, a damaged or obstructed track, poor alignment, a faulty handle or lock, or worn rubber beading. Kutbi checks the complete sliding system before recommending repair or a compatible replacement.
