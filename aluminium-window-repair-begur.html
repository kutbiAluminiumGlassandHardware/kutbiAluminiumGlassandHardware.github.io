What we repair

Sliding windows may become hard to move because of worn rollers or wheels, track problems, loose handles, faulty locks or damaged rubber beading. Kutbi assesses the existing window and focuses on the component that needs attention.

  • Aluminium sliding window repair
  • Window roller replacement
  • Window wheel replacement
  • Window lock replacement
  • Window handle replacement
  • Rubber beading replacement
  • Track and alignment checks
  • Aluminium sliding door hardware repair

Repair-first approach

If the existing aluminium frame is sound, replacing a worn roller, wheel, lock, handle or seal may restore the window without a full replacement. Where a frame or other major component is unsuitable for repair, we can explain the available replacement options.
