Track + wheel + alignment diagnosis

Why We Diagnose the Whole Sliding System

A handle or lock can appear to be the problem when worn wheels, rollers, track condition or poor alignment prevent the shutter from reaching the correct closing position. Similarly, replacing a wheel alone may not solve a problem caused by track or alignment issues.

1. Symptom Check

We understand whether the window or door is stuck, noisy, dragging, difficult to close or not locking.

2. Wheel & Roller Check

Inspect the sliding hardware and determine whether compatible wheel or roller replacement is required.

3. Track & Alignment Check

Check accessible track condition, shutter position and alignment where they affect movement.

4. Hardware Check

Inspect handles, locks, fixing points and other related operating hardware.

Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Handles Window & Door Repair

  1. Understand the problem: We note the movement, closing, locking or hardware symptom.
  2. Inspect the existing system: Aluminium or uPVC frame, shutter, wheels, rollers, track, alignment and hardware are checked as applicable.
  3. Diagnose the actual cause: We identify whether the problem is with wheels, rollers, track, alignment, handle, lock, beading or another related component.
  4. Repair or adjust: Suitable servicing, adjustment or repair is carried out where possible.
  5. Replace compatible parts: Worn wheels, rollers, handles, locks or rubber beading can be replaced when compatible parts are available.
  6. Test the result: Movement, closing, alignment and locking operation are checked after the work.

Repair requirements vary by aluminium or uPVC profile, existing hardware and installation condition, so the final scope is confirmed from the actual window or door.
