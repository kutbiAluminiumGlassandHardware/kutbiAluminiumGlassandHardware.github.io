Common Window Repair Problems

Stuck or difficult window

Inspection for windows that are jammed, dragging, noisy or difficult to open and close.

Wheel & roller problems

Replacement of worn compatible sliding wheels or rollers where the existing system can be repaired.

Track & alignment problems

Inspection, servicing and suitable adjustment when the track or alignment affects operation.

Handle & lock problems

Repair or replacement of compatible window and door handles, locks and related hardware.

Rubber beading

Replacement of worn or deteriorated rubber beading for suitable existing windows and doors.

Broken glass

Suitable broken window or door glass replacement after checking the opening and glass requirement.
