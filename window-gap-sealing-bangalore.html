Find the Cause of a Window Gap Before Sealing It

A gap around an aluminium or uPVC window may be related to worn rubber beading, shutter alignment, closing problems or the condition of the existing frame and sliding system. Kutbi checks the source of the gap before recommending suitable sealing work.

Hardened, loose or damaged beading can leave unwanted gaps around a window.

Window Gap Sealing Repair Process

  1. Identify where the gap is visible and whether it changes when the window opens or closes.
  2. Inspect the surrounding beading, seals and frame/shutter condition.
  3. Check alignment and, for sliding windows, the relevant wheels, rollers and track.
  4. Determine whether the issue can be addressed through suitable sealing, beading replacement or related repair.
  5. Carry out the appropriate repair without assuming complete window replacement is necessary.
  6. Test closing and check the repaired area after the work.

Repair First — Seal the Actual Problem

A visible window gap does not automatically mean the complete window needs replacement. Where the existing system remains suitable, Kutbi focuses on the affected seal, beading, alignment or compatible component first.
