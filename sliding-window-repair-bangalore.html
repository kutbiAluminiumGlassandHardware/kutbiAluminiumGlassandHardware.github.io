Repair for Stuck, Noisy & Difficult Sliding Windows

Kutbi provides repair-first service for suitable aluminium and uPVC sliding windows in Bengaluru. We inspect the existing shutter and sliding mechanism, identify the likely cause and recommend the appropriate repair or compatible hardware replacement.

Typical problems include worn wheels or rollers, track issues, poor alignment, loose handles, faulty locks and shutters that have become hard to move.

Sliding Window Problems We Handle

Stuck or hard-to-move shutter

uPVC Sliding Window Repair

uPVC sliding windows can become difficult to operate when compatible rollers wear out, tracks collect debris, shutters lose alignment or hardware becomes damaged. Kutbi provides suitable on-site repair and wheel or roller replacement after inspection.

  • uPVC sliding-window wheel and roller replacement
  • Stuck and difficult-to-move uPVC window service
  • Track inspection and servicing
  • Sliding shutter alignment and adjustment
  • uPVC window handle and lock replacement
  • General sliding-window servicing

How Sliding Window Repair Works

  1. Inspect the window, shutter, wheels or rollers and track.
  2. Identify the cause of difficult or uneven movement.
  3. Recommend repair, servicing or compatible component replacement.
  4. Replace suitable worn hardware where required.
  5. Adjust alignment and test smooth opening and closing.
