Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Window Repair

Kutbi Aluminium Glass & Hardware provides repair-first service for suitable aluminium and uPVC sliding windows in Bengaluru. We inspect the existing window, identify the mechanical or hardware problem and recommend the appropriate repair or replacement based on the site condition.

Common issues include worn window wheels and rollers, damaged or dirty tracks, poor alignment, loose handles, faulty locks and sliding shutters that have become hard to move.

Common Sliding Window Problems We Repair

Stuck or hard-to-slide window

Inspection and servicing for shutters that require excessive force or no longer slide smoothly.

Window wheel replacement

Replacement of worn or damaged sliding wheels where the existing system is suitable for repair.

Window roller replacement

Roller replacement for aluminium and uPVC sliding windows when worn rollers affect movement.

Track repair & servicing

Track inspection, cleaning, adjustment and suitable repair when track condition affects sliding.

Handle replacement

Suitable window handle replacement for damaged, loose or malfunctioning handles.

Lock repair & replacement

Inspection and suitable lock or locking-hardware replacement when the window does not lock correctly.

How Our Sliding Window Repair Works

  1. Inspect the window, shutter, rollers or wheels and track.
  2. Identify the cause of difficult or uneven movement.
  3. Recommend repair, servicing or replacement of the affected component.
  4. Replace suitable worn hardware where required.
  5. Adjust alignment and test smooth opening and closing.
