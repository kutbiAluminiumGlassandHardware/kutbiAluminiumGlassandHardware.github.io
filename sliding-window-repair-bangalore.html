Track + Wheel + Alignment Diagnosis

A sliding window should not be repaired by changing one component blindly. Kutbi checks the main moving parts together:

  • Track: check for dirt, wear, damage or conditions affecting the shutter path.
  • Wheels and rollers: inspect for wear, damage, uneven movement or loss of smooth operation.
  • Alignment: check whether the shutter is sitting correctly and travelling evenly along the track.
  • Hardware: inspect handles, locks and related components if closing or locking is affected.
  • Beading: check deteriorated rubber beading when it is contributing to the window's condition or sealing problem.

Detailed Sliding Window Repair Process

  1. Understand the symptom: identify whether the window is stuck, heavy, noisy, dragging, misaligned or difficult to lock.
  2. Inspect the sliding system: check shutter movement, wheels or rollers, track, alignment and relevant hardware.
  3. Find the actual fault: determine whether servicing, adjustment or component replacement is appropriate.
  4. Carry out the repair: replace compatible worn wheels, rollers, handles, locks or beading and service or adjust the track where suitable.
  5. Test the result: check smooth opening, closing, alignment and locking before completing the work.
