Window Glass Replacement: What Kutbi Checks First

  1. Inspect whether the glass is broken, cracked, loose or otherwise damaged.
  2. Check the existing aluminium or uPVC frame and glass seating.
  3. Check visible rubber beading and sealing conditions.
  4. Look for gaps, alignment or closing problems that may affect the completed window.
  5. Assess the practical replacement requirements for the existing frame.
  6. Install suitable replacement glass where appropriate and check the completed window condition.

This repair-first assessment helps avoid assuming that a complete window replacement is necessary when the existing frame may still be suitable.

Window Glass Replacement FAQs

Can broken glass be replaced without replacing the whole window?

In suitable cases, replacement glass can be assessed for the existing aluminium or uPVC frame.

Can cracked window glass be replaced?

Yes, suitable cracked glass can be assessed for replacement based on the existing frame and glass requirements.

Can loose window glass be fixed?

The glass seating, rubber beading and frame condition should be checked first to determine the suitable solution.

Do aluminium and uPVC windows need different glass checks?

The surrounding frame and sealing system can differ, so Kutbi assesses the existing window type and compatible replacement requirements.

Does glass replacement fix window leakage?

Not necessarily. Leakage can also involve rubber beading, gaps, alignment or other window conditions, so the cause should be checked.

Can I send a photo first?

Yes. A clear photo of the full window and damaged or loose glass can help with an initial assessment.
