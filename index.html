REPAIR FAQ

Questions Customers Ask About Window & Door Repairs

Quick answers to common repair questions.

Do you repair aluminium sliding windows?

Yes. Kutbi repairs existing aluminium sliding windows, including wheel, roller, track, alignment, handle and lock problems.

Do you repair uPVC sliding windows and doors?

Yes. We provide uPVC window and door repair for stuck, jammed or difficult-to-operate units and related hardware problems.

Can you replace window rollers and wheels?

Yes. We replace worn or damaged window and sliding-door wheels or rollers where suitable, followed by alignment and operation checks.

Can you repair a window that is stuck or difficult to slide?

Yes. We inspect the sliding mechanism and address suitable wheel, roller, track, alignment or hardware problems.

Can you replace old or damaged mosquito mesh?

Yes. Kutbi provides mosquito mesh repair and replacement for suitable aluminium and uPVC windows and doors.

Can I send photos before requesting a repair?

Yes. Sending photos on WhatsApp helps us understand the window, door or hardware problem before the visit.
