Signs Your Sliding Window May Need New Wheels or Rollers

  • Sliding window is suddenly difficult or heavy to move
  • Window makes scraping, grinding or rolling noises
  • Shutter drags or catches while sliding
  • Window repeatedly gets stuck
  • Wheel or roller is visibly damaged or seized
  • Shutter sits unevenly on the track
  • Window movement has become rough or jerky
  • Window no longer stays properly aligned with the track

Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Window Wheel Replacement in Bangalore

Different aluminium and uPVC windows use different wheel and roller designs. The correct replacement depends on the existing shutter, frame, hardware and available compatible component. Kutbi checks the actual window before carrying out suitable replacement work.

A wheel replacement may improve the sliding operation, but the track and alignment also matter. If the track is damaged, obstructed or uneven, replacing the wheel alone may not solve the problem. That is why the sliding mechanism is checked as a whole where required.

Our Roller & Wheel Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the window and understand the movement problem.
  2. Check the existing wheels, rollers, track and shutter alignment.
  3. Identify the suitable compatible replacement component where required.
  4. Remove the affected wheel or roller when replacement is practical.
  5. Fit the compatible hardware and adjust the shutter.
  6. Test sliding, closing and alignment before completion.

When the Problem Is Not the Window Roller

A difficult sliding window can also be caused by a damaged or obstructed track, incorrect alignment, worn hardware or another mechanical issue. Kutbi checks these related causes before recommending roller or wheel replacement.
