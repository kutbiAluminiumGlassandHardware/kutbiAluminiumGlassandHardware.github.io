Signs Your Sliding Window or Door May Need New Wheels or Rollers

  • Sliding window or door is suddenly difficult or heavy to move
  • Scraping, grinding or rolling noises during operation
  • Shutter or door panel drags or catches while sliding
  • Window or door repeatedly gets stuck
  • Wheel or roller is visibly damaged or seized
  • Shutter or door panel sits unevenly on the track
  • Movement has become rough, jerky or noisy
  • Window or door no longer stays properly aligned with the track
  • Closing or locking becomes difficult because the panel is sitting incorrectly

Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Window & Door Wheel Replacement in Bangalore

Different aluminium and uPVC sliding windows and doors use different wheel and roller designs. The correct replacement depends on the existing shutter, frame, hardware and compatible component. Kutbi checks the actual system before carrying out suitable replacement work.

Track & Alignment Diagnosis

A wheel replacement may improve movement, but the track and alignment also matter. Kutbi checks the wheels, rollers, track, shutter or door panel and frame alignment to identify the actual cause of the problem. If the track is damaged, obstructed or uneven, replacing the wheel alone may not solve the issue.

Our Detailed Roller & Wheel Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door and understand the operating problem.
  2. Check the existing wheels, rollers, track, shutter or door panel and alignment.
  3. Diagnose whether the main problem is the wheel, roller, track, alignment or related hardware.
  4. Identify a suitable compatible replacement component where required.
  5. Remove the affected wheel or roller when replacement is practical.
  6. Fit the compatible hardware and adjust the shutter or door panel.
  7. Check track alignment and make suitable adjustments.
  8. Test sliding, closing and locking before completion.

When the Problem Is Not the Window or Door Roller

A difficult sliding system can also be caused by a damaged or obstructed track, incorrect alignment, worn hardware or another mechanical issue. Kutbi checks these related causes before recommending roller or wheel replacement.
