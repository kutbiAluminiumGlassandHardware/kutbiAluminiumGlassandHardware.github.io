  • Window feels heavy while sliding
  • Roller or wheel is visibly damaged
  • Window does not stay aligned with the track
  • Sliding window gets stuck repeatedly

Aluminium & uPVC Window Roller Wheel Replacement in Bangalore

Window rollers are working parts that carry the weight of a sliding shutter along its track. With regular use, wheels may wear down, seize, crack or lose alignment. Replacing the appropriate roller can restore smoother movement without replacing the complete window.

Kutbi checks the window shutter, rollers, wheels and track before carrying out suitable repair work. The exact replacement depends on the existing window design and hardware.

Our Roller Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the sliding window and identify the operating problem.
  2. Check the existing rollers, wheels and track condition.
  3. Remove the damaged roller or wheel where replacement is suitable.
  4. Fit compatible replacement hardware.
  5. Adjust the shutter alignment and test the sliding movement.

When a Track Check Is Also Needed

A worn roller is not always the only reason a window becomes difficult to slide. Dirt, damaged tracks and alignment problems can also affect operation. For this reason, Kutbi checks the complete sliding mechanism where required.
