Glass Repair: What Kutbi Checks Before Replacement

  1. Inspect whether the glass is broken, cracked, loose or otherwise damaged.
  2. Check the existing aluminium or uPVC frame and how the glass is seated.
  3. Check visible rubber beading and sealing conditions.
  4. Look for gaps, leakage or alignment problems that may affect the window.
  5. Assess the practical requirements for suitable glass repair or replacement.
  6. Complete the work and check the window condition after installation or repair.

The goal is to repair or replace the affected glass or related component where practical rather than automatically recommending a complete new window.
