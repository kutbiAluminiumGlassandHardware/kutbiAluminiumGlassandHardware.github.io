Glass Repair FAQs

Can you replace broken glass in an aluminium sliding window?

Yes. We can replace broken or cracked glass in suitable existing aluminium sliding window frames and can also repair related rollers, tracks, locks, handles and beading when required.

Do you replace broken glass in uPVC windows and doors?

Yes. We provide glass replacement for suitable existing uPVC windows and doors, including sliding and openable systems.

Can broken glass replacement be combined with window repair?

Yes. If the frame or hardware also has a problem, glass replacement can be combined with wheel or roller replacement, track repair, lock repair, handle replacement or rubber beading replacement.
