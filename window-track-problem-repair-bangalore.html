Specialist track diagnosis

What a Sliding Window Track Problem Can Actually Be

A sliding window or door that feels like it has a track problem may also have worn wheels or rollers, incorrect alignment, or related hardware issues. Kutbi checks the complete existing system before recommending a targeted repair.

Track wear or damage

Worn, bent or damaged track sections can affect the path of the shutter or door panel and cause dragging or sticking.

Track obstruction

Dust, debris or an obstruction can interfere with smooth sliding. The track is inspected before more extensive work is recommended.

Wheel or roller wear

Worn wheels can make the panel sit incorrectly and create symptoms that feel like a track problem.

Repair-first approach

Track Repair Before Complete Window Replacement

Not every difficult sliding window needs a new window. Depending on the existing system and its condition, Kutbi may clean, adjust, align, repair or replace a compatible component. The aim is to identify the actual operating fault first and avoid unnecessary complete replacement.

If the track itself is not the main cause, Kutbi can assess the related wheels and rollers, handles and locks or rubber beading.

Track Repair: Practical Diagnostic Checklist

  1. Identify whether the problem is sticking, dragging, scraping, noise or difficult closing.
  2. Inspect the track for wear, damage, obstruction and uneven sections.
  3. Check wheels and rollers for wear, seizure or incorrect positioning.
  4. Check shutter/panel alignment and relevant handle or lock operation.
  5. Carry out suitable cleaning, adjustment, repair or compatible component replacement.
  6. Test smooth sliding, closing and locking after the work.
