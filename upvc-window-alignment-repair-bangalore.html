uPVC Window Alignment Repair

For uPVC window alignment repair, Kutbi checks the existing window for sash or panel position, hinges or sliding hardware, rollers, handles, locks and sealing before suitable adjustment or repair.

Common Problems We Check

Window rubbing or dragging

Misalignment can make an existing uPVC window difficult to operate.

Window not closing properly

Panel position and hardware alignment can prevent correct closing.

Lock not engaging

Alignment can stop locking points from meeting correctly.

Sliding movement problems

For suitable sliding windows, rollers, tracks and panel position are checked together.

Uneven sealing

Poor alignment can create uneven gaps around the frame or sash.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the existing system and related components before recommending suitable repair or compatible replacement. The aim is to identify the cause of the problem rather than treating only the visible symptom.

  1. Inspect the reported problem and existing system.
  2. Check related hardware, frame, glass, mesh, sealing or sliding parts.
  3. Identify the likely cause and suitable repair.
  4. Carry out appropriate repair or compatible replacement.
  5. Test operation and finish checks after the work.
