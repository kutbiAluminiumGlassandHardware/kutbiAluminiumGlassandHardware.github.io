Window repair for Hulimavu homes and commercial properties

Aluminium sliding windows can remain useful for many years, but their moving hardware is exposed to daily wear. When rollers become worn or tracks collect dirt, a panel may become heavy or noisy. A damaged handle or lock can also make an otherwise good window inconvenient or less secure.

Kutbi Aluminium Glass & Hardware provides repair-first support for suitable aluminium windows and doors. We inspect the existing frame and hardware and recommend a repair or replacement component when appropriate.
