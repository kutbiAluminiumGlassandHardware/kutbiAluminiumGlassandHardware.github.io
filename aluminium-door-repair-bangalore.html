Track, Wheel & Alignment Diagnosis

A difficult aluminium sliding door does not always need a new wheel. Kutbi checks the complete sliding system to identify whether the cause is a worn wheel or roller, track condition, obstruction, alignment, hardware or another operating issue.

The diagnosis focuses on the actual door: wheel movement, roller condition, accessible track, panel alignment, closing position, handle and lock operation. After identifying the cause, we carry out suitable servicing, adjustment or compatible part replacement.

Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Repairs an Aluminium Sliding Door

  1. Inspect: Check the door, wheels, rollers, accessible track, alignment, handle, lock and rubber beading.
  2. Diagnose: Identify what is causing difficult movement, noise, rubbing, poor closing or locking.
  3. Service: Clean, adjust or service the suitable sliding mechanism where required.
  4. Repair or replace: Replace compatible worn wheels, rollers, handles, locks or rubber beading where necessary.
  5. Align: Adjust the door position and movement where suitable.
  6. Test: Check smooth sliding, opening, closing, alignment and locking operation.

Repair requirements vary by aluminium profile, door design and existing hardware, so compatibility and final scope are confirmed from the actual door.
