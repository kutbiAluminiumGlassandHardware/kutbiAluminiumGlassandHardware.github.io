Track, Wheel & Alignment Diagnosis Before Repair

A difficult aluminium sliding door does not always need a new wheel. Kutbi checks the complete sliding system to identify whether the cause is a worn wheel or roller, track condition, obstruction, alignment, hardware or another operating issue.

The diagnosis focuses on wheel movement, roller condition, accessible track, panel alignment, closing position, handle and lock operation. Where the issue involves more than one component, suitable repair can include wheel or roller replacement, track servicing, alignment adjustment, handle or lock repair, or rubber beading replacement.

How Kutbi Repairs an Aluminium Sliding Door

  1. Inspect: Check the door, wheels, rollers, accessible track, alignment, handle, lock and rubber beading.
  2. Understand the symptom: Identify whether the door is stuck, dragging, noisy, rubbing, difficult to close or difficult to lock.
  3. Diagnose: Identify what is causing difficult movement, noise, rubbing, poor closing or locking.
  4. Service: Clean, adjust or service the suitable sliding mechanism where required.
  5. Repair or replace: Replace compatible worn wheels, rollers, handles, locks or rubber beading where necessary.
  6. Test: Check smooth sliding, opening, closing, alignment and locking operation.

Repair requirements vary by aluminium profile, door design and existing hardware, so compatibility and final scope are confirmed from the actual door.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my aluminium sliding door not sliding?

An aluminium sliding door can become difficult to move because of worn wheels or rollers, track problems, dirt or misalignment. Kutbi checks the sliding mechanism and repairs or replaces the affected component where suitable.

How do I fix a stuck aluminium sliding door?

A stuck aluminium sliding door should be checked for wheel, roller, track and alignment problems. Kutbi identifies the cause and carries out the appropriate repair.

Why is my aluminium sliding door hard to open?

Worn wheels or rollers, track problems, alignment issues or damaged hardware can make an aluminium sliding door difficult to open. Kutbi checks the existing door to identify the required repair.

Can aluminium sliding door rollers or wheels be replaced?

Yes. Worn or damaged aluminium sliding door wheels and rollers can often be replaced with compatible hardware without replacing the complete door.

Can an aluminium sliding door track be repaired?

Depending on the condition and extent of damage, the track may be repairable or adjustable. Kutbi checks the track, wheels, rollers and alignment before recommending the suitable repair.
