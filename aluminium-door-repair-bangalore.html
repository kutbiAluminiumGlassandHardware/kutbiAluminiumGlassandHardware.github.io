Why Is My Aluminium Sliding Door Difficult to Move?

An aluminium sliding door can become difficult to operate when wheels or rollers wear out, the track needs servicing, the door becomes misaligned, or the handle and lock develop faults. Kutbi checks the existing system and recommends suitable repair, adjustment or compatible part replacement based on the actual door.

For aluminium windows, see Aluminium Window Repair Bangalore. For general sliding windows, see Sliding Window Repair Bangalore.

How Kutbi Handles Aluminium Door Repair

  1. Inspect: Check the door, wheels, rollers, track, alignment, handle and lock.
  2. Identify: Determine the likely cause of difficult movement, closing or locking.
  3. Repair or replace: Carry out suitable servicing, adjustment or compatible hardware replacement.
  4. Test: Check movement, closing, alignment and locking after the work.

Repair requirements vary by door design and existing hardware, so compatibility and final scope are confirmed from the actual door.
