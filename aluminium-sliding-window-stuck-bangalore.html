Common Causes of a Stuck Aluminium Sliding Window

Worn wheels or rollers

Worn rollers can prevent the sash from moving freely and may make the window feel completely stuck.

Track damage or obstruction

Track wear, damage or obstruction can stop smooth movement and should be checked with the rollers.

Alignment problems

An uneven sash or frame can cause rubbing and resistance, especially near one part of the travel.

Hardware problems

Handles, locks and other compatible hardware can also interfere with correct window operation.

How Kutbi Repairs a Stuck Aluminium Sliding Window

  1. Understand how and where the window is stuck.
  2. Inspect wheels or rollers and the track.
  3. Check sash and frame alignment.
  4. Inspect handles, locks and relevant hardware.
  5. Check glass and rubber beading where relevant.
  6. Recommend suitable repair or component replacement.
  7. Test movement and closing after the work.

Warning Signs Before a Window Gets Fully Stuck

Dragging, scraping, repeated sticking, uneven movement, unusual noise and increasing effort when opening or closing are useful signs to have the system checked.

Repair or Full Window Replacement?

A stuck sliding window does not automatically require a new frame. If the existing frame and system are suitable, repairing or replacing the affected component may restore operation. Full replacement can be considered when the existing system is unsuitable or beyond practical repair.

What Kutbi Checks When an Aluminium Sliding Window Is Stuck

  1. Check whether the sash is completely stuck, hard to slide or scraping.
  2. Inspect rollers or wheels for wear, damage and uneven travel.
  3. Check the aluminium sliding track for obstruction, wear or damage.
  4. Check sash and frame alignment and rubbing points.
  5. Test handle and lock engagement after checking sliding movement.
  6. Check visible rubber beading, gaps and glass condition where relevant.
  7. Repair or replace the affected component where practical and test sliding and closing.

A stuck aluminium sliding window does not automatically mean the complete window needs replacement. The repair-first approach identifies the actual cause first.
