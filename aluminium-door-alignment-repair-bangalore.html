Door movement problems

Aluminium Door Alignment Problems

When an aluminium sliding door becomes misaligned, it may drag, rub against the frame, move unevenly or stop closing correctly. We check the complete door movement before recommending a suitable repair.

Dragging or Rubbing

A door that scrapes the frame, floor area or track may need alignment, roller or track inspection.

Door Not Closing

Gaps, frame contact and misalignment can prevent a sliding door from reaching its correct closed position.

Uneven Movement

One side moving differently from the other can indicate roller height, track or alignment issues.

Inspection before adjustment

Roller, Track & Frame Check

Roller Check

We inspect suitable door rollers for wear, movement, height and smooth operation before alignment work.

Track Check

The track is checked for dirt, obstruction, damage and conditions that can affect door alignment.

Frame & Hardware Check

Frame position, handles, locks and related hardware are checked when they can contribute to closing or alignment problems.

Repair-first approach

Aluminium Door Alignment Adjustment

Where suitable, we adjust the door and related hardware to restore smoother movement and correct positioning. If a worn roller, damaged track or other part is causing the problem, we explain the required repair before replacement.

Our Alignment Repair Process

  1. Understand the door movement or closing problem.
  2. Inspect rollers, track, frame and related hardware.
  3. Identify the likely cause of misalignment.
  4. Carry out suitable adjustment or repair.
  5. Test sliding, alignment and closing after the work.
Troubleshooting

Common Aluminium Door Alignment Problems

Common symptoms include dragging, rubbing, uneven sliding, excessive gaps, difficulty closing and a door that sits out of position. These symptoms can have different causes, so inspection is important.

Repair vs Replacement

Alignment problems do not automatically mean the complete door needs replacement. We first check whether adjustment, roller, track, hardware or another suitable repair can resolve the issue.

Frequently asked questions

FAQs

Can an aluminium sliding door be realigned?

In suitable cases, yes. The rollers, track, frame position and related hardware should be inspected first.

Why is my aluminium door dragging?

Possible causes include roller wear, track problems, obstruction or alignment issues. An inspection helps identify the cause.

Does alignment repair require a new door?

Not necessarily. Suitable repair or adjustment may solve the problem when the existing system can be repaired.
