For houses, apartments and small businesses

Jayanagar has a mix of independent homes, apartments and commercial properties. We assess each existing window according to its frame, glass, hardware and site condition. Where a repair is practical, replacing the worn component can restore smooth operation without replacing the entire frame.

Why use a repair-focused window service?

A stuck sliding window may be caused by a small component such as a roller or wheel. A lock problem may need only a compatible replacement. Kutbi focuses on identifying the actual problem and providing a suitable repair or replacement solution.

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair old aluminium windows?

Yes, provided the frame and other components are suitable for repair and compatible replacement parts are available.

Can you replace window rollers in Jayanagar?

Yes. Roller and wheel replacement is one of the common services for sliding aluminium windows.

Do you replace window locks and handles?

Yes, suitable locks, latches and handles can be replaced after checking the existing system.

Should I replace the whole window if it is difficult to slide?

Not necessarily. Worn rollers, wheels, track problems or alignment can sometimes be the cause, so inspection is useful before deciding on replacement.
