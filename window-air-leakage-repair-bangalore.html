Find the Cause of Window Air Leakage Before Replacing the Window

Air leakage around an aluminium or uPVC window can come from worn rubber beading, gaps, poor closing, alignment or related window conditions. Kutbi checks the existing system before recommending suitable sealing or repair.

Window Air Leakage Repair Process

  1. Identify where air is entering and when the problem occurs.
  2. Inspect visible beading, seals, gaps and shutter position.
  3. Check alignment and relevant track, wheel or roller components where applicable.
  4. Identify the likely source instead of assuming complete window replacement is required.
  5. Carry out suitable sealing, beading, adjustment or compatible component repair.
  6. Check closing and sealing condition after the work.

Repair First — Replace Only When Necessary

Air leakage does not automatically mean the complete window needs replacement. Where the existing frame and operating system remain suitable, Kutbi can focus on the affected seal, gap, alignment or compatible component.
