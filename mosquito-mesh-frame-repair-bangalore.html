Repair or Replace the Mosquito Mesh Frame?

Repair may be suitable when the existing frame remains structurally usable and the problem is related to fitting, fixing or a minor frame issue. If the frame is badly damaged, bent or unsuitable for secure fitting, replacement may be the better practical option. Kutbi checks the condition before advising.
