Repair or Replace the Mosquito Mesh Frame?

Repair is considered first when the existing frame remains structurally usable and the problem is related to fitting, fixing, alignment or a minor frame issue. A frame may need replacement when it is badly damaged, significantly bent, structurally unsuitable or unable to provide secure fitting.

Repair May Be Suitable

Existing frame is usable, fixing can be restored, alignment can be corrected, or the problem is limited enough for practical adjustment or refixing.

Replacement May Be Suitable

Frame is badly damaged, bent, weakened or no longer suitable for secure fitting and reliable use.

Kutbi checks the condition before recommending repair or replacement rather than replacing a usable frame unnecessarily.
