Window Water Leakage Repair Process

  1. Identify where and when water enters.
  2. Inspect visible seals, beading, gaps and window position.
  3. Check alignment and relevant sliding-system components where applicable.
  4. Identify the likely source rather than assuming the complete window needs replacement.
  5. Carry out suitable sealing, adjustment, beading or compatible component repair.
  6. Check the window's closing and sealing condition after the work.

Repair First — Replace Only When Necessary

A leaking window may be repairable even when the problem looks serious. Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and considers the condition of the existing system before recommending larger replacement work.

If the leakage is connected with air gaps, also see Window Air Leakage Repair and Window Gap Sealing.

Common Causes of Window Water Leakage

Water entry can be related to worn rubber beading, gaps around the opening, damaged or poorly seated seals, alignment problems or other conditions around the existing window system.
