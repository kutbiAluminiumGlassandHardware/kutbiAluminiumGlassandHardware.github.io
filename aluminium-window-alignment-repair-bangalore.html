Signs of Aluminium Window Misalignment

Dragging or rubbing

The sash may scrape or feel unusually heavy at one point while sliding.

Uneven movement

The window may sit unevenly or move with resistance instead of travelling smoothly.

Window not closing properly

A misaligned sash can prevent the window from meeting the frame correctly.

Gaps or inconsistent contact

Uneven positioning can create visible gaps or affect normal closing and locking.

Signs Your Window May Have an Alignment Problem

  • The sliding shutter drags, rubs or moves unevenly.
  • The window does not reach the correct closing position.
  • A gap appears unevenly around the closed window.
  • The handle or lock does not engage easily because the shutter position is off.
  • One side of the shutter appears higher or lower than the other.
  • Window movement changed after wear developed in wheels, rollers or tracks.

Alignment symptoms can overlap with component problems, so Kutbi checks the complete operating system before deciding what needs adjustment or replacement.

Window Alignment Repair Process

  1. Identify whether the problem affects sliding, closing, locking or visible gaps.
  2. Check the shutter/frame position and visible alignment.
  3. Inspect wheels and rollers where the window is a sliding system.
  4. Check the track for wear, obstruction or related condition.
  5. Check handle/lock engagement and surrounding sealing where relevant.
  6. Carry out suitable alignment adjustment or related component repair and test the window.

Window Alignment Repair Questions

Why is my sliding window not aligned?

Possible causes include wheel or roller wear, track condition, shutter position or related hardware. The system should be checked before assuming one cause.

Can an aluminium sliding window be realigned?

Suitable existing aluminium sliding windows can be assessed for alignment and related operating problems.

Can alignment cause a window not to close?

Yes. A shutter that is out of position may not reach the correct closing or locking position.

Can alignment problems cause gaps?

They can contribute to uneven gaps, although beading, seals and other conditions should also be checked.

Does alignment repair require a new window?

Not automatically. Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and checks whether the existing window can be adjusted or repaired.

Can I send a photo first?

Yes. A clear photo of the complete window and the alignment or closing problem can help with an initial assessment.
