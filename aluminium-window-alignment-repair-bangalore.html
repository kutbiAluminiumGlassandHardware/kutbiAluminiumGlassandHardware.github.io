Signs of Aluminium Window Misalignment

Dragging or rubbing

The sash may scrape or feel unusually heavy at one point while sliding.

Uneven movement

The window may sit unevenly or move with resistance instead of travelling smoothly.

Window not closing properly

A misaligned sash can prevent the window from meeting the frame correctly.

Gaps or inconsistent contact

Uneven positioning can create visible gaps or affect normal closing and locking.

Alignment Adjustment

Where the existing system is suitable for repair, Kutbi adjusts the relevant components and checks the window's movement, closing and operation. Alignment work is carried out with the condition of the rollers, track and frame in mind.

Aluminium Window Alignment Repair Process

  1. Understand where the window drags, rubs or fails to close.
  2. Inspect sash, frame, rollers and track.
  3. Check handles, locks and related hardware where needed.
  4. Identify whether the issue is adjustment, component wear or another repair requirement.
  5. Carry out suitable alignment adjustment or component repair.
  6. Test sliding, closing and locking operation.

Warning Signs to Check Early

Increasing effort, scraping sounds, repeated sticking, uneven gaps, a sash that appears tilted, or a window that no longer closes smoothly are useful reasons to have the system inspected.

Repair or Full Window Replacement?

Misalignment does not automatically mean the entire aluminium window needs replacement. If the frame and operating system remain suitable, adjustment or replacement of the affected component may restore normal operation.

Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and considers full replacement when the existing system is unsuitable or beyond practical repair.

FAQs

Why does my aluminium sliding window sit unevenly?

Possible causes include roller wear, track problems, sash alignment or related hardware issues.

Can an aluminium sliding window be realigned?

Where the existing system is suitable, the relevant components can be adjusted and the window tested for normal movement.

Can rollers cause alignment problems?

Yes. Worn or damaged rollers can affect sash position and movement, so they should be checked as part of the diagnosis.

Does alignment repair always require replacement?

No. Kutbi checks whether adjustment or component repair can solve the problem before considering full replacement.
