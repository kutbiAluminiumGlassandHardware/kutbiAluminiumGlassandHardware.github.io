Door handle problems

Aluminium Door Handle Problems

A loose, broken or difficult-to-operate handle can affect how an aluminium sliding door is used. We inspect the handle and connected hardware before recommending a suitable repair.

Loose Handle

A handle that moves excessively may need fixing, adjustment or inspection of its mounting points.

Broken Handle

Damaged handles can be checked for a compatible replacement suitable for the existing aluminium door system.

Stiff or Difficult Handle

A stiff handle may be related to the handle itself, lock mechanism, alignment or other door hardware.

Handle Not Operating Correctly

If the handle does not operate the latch or lock correctly, the connected mechanism should be inspected rather than replacing parts unnecessarily.

Hardware inspection

Handle & Lock Check

We check the handle together with the connected lock or latch where applicable. Door alignment, movement and hardware condition can affect handle operation.

Handle Fixing Check

Mounting points, screws and surrounding hardware are checked to determine whether the existing handle can be secured or adjusted.

Compatible Handle Replacement

When replacement is required, we check compatibility with the existing aluminium door system instead of assuming every handle will fit.

Repair-first approach

Aluminium Door Handle Repair Process

  1. Understand the handle or door-operation problem.
  2. Inspect the handle, fixing points and connected hardware.
  3. Check related lock, latch and door alignment where relevant.
  4. Carry out suitable adjustment or repair.
  5. Use a compatible replacement when the existing handle is not suitable for repair.
  6. Test handle and door operation after the work.
Troubleshooting

Common Aluminium Door Handle Problems

Common symptoms include a loose handle, broken handle, stiff operation, handle movement without proper latch action, or difficulty using the door hardware. Different symptoms can have different causes, so inspection is important.

Repair vs Replacement

A faulty handle does not automatically mean the complete door needs replacement. Where suitable, we first look at fixing, adjustment or compatible hardware replacement.

FAQs

Can a loose aluminium door handle be repaired?

In suitable cases, the handle can be secured or adjusted after checking its fixing points and connected hardware.

Can you replace a broken aluminium door handle?

Yes, when a compatible replacement is available for the existing door system.

Why is my door handle stiff?

It may be caused by the handle, lock or latch, door alignment or another hardware issue. Inspection helps identify the cause.
