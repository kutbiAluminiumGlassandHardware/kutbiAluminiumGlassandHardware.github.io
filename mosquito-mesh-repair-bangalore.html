Repair or Replace Mosquito Mesh?

Repair can be suitable when the existing mesh and fixing system remain usable and the problem is limited to a repairable area, loose fixing or fitting issue.

Replacement may be more practical when the mesh is extensively torn or worn, the fixing system has deteriorated, or the existing installation cannot provide secure coverage. Kutbi checks the condition before recommending either option.
