Window and door repair services

For a sliding window that is stuck, dragging, noisy or difficult to lock, Kutbi can inspect the wheels or rollers, track, alignment, handle and locking hardware. Where the frame is suitable, targeted repair can avoid unnecessary full replacement.

  • uPVC sliding window repair

  • Lock and handle replacement
  • Rubber beading replacement
  • Broken glass replacement

New and replacement aluminium or uPVC windows and doors are handled according to measurements and the existing opening. Mosquito mesh installation and replacement is available for suitable window and door openings.
