What Kutbi Checks When an Aluminium Window Is Stuck

  1. Identify whether the window is stuck while opening, sliding or closing.
  2. Inspect rollers or wheels where the window uses a sliding system.
  3. Check the track for obstruction, wear or damage.
  4. Check sash and frame alignment and visible rubbing points.
  5. Test handle and lock engagement where applicable.
  6. Check visible rubber beading, gaps and glass condition where relevant.
  7. Repair or replace the affected component where practical and test operation.

A stuck aluminium window does not automatically require complete replacement. The repair-first approach identifies the actual cause before recommending the next step.

Aluminium Window Stuck Repair FAQs

Why is my aluminium window stuck?

Possible causes include worn rollers, track obstruction or damage, misalignment and hardware problems.

Why is my aluminium sliding window hard to move?

Rollers, wheels, track condition and alignment are common areas to inspect.

Can aluminium window rollers be replaced?

Suitable worn or damaged rollers can be assessed for replacement without automatically replacing the complete window.

Can an aluminium window track be repaired?

The track can be checked for obstruction, wear and damage together with the rollers before deciding on the suitable repair.

Why is my aluminium window stuck and not closing?

The cause may involve rollers, track condition, alignment or handle and lock engagement.

Do I need a new aluminium window?

Not automatically. The existing frame and affected components can be assessed first for suitable repair or replacement.
