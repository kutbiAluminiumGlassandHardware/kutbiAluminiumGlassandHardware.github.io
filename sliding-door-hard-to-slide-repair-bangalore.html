Sliding Door Hard to Slide Repair

Repair for sliding doors that are difficult to move because of suitable wheel, roller, track or alignment problems.

This is a repair/replacement service for existing aluminium or uPVC windows and doors. We inspect the existing system and replace or adjust suitable compatible parts where required.

What we check

Existing hardware

We inspect the existing frame, hardware and operating condition before recommending a replacement.

Related mechanism

Where relevant, we check wheels, rollers, tracks, alignment, handles and locks so the real cause is not missed.

Repair or replacement

Where suitable, the faulty component is repaired, adjusted or replaced with a compatible part and then tested.

Repair-first service

We focus on restoring the existing window or door where practical rather than recommending unnecessary complete replacement.
