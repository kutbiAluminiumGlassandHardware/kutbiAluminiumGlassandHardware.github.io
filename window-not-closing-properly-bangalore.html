What Kutbi Checks When a Window Will Not Close

  • Shutter position: whether the window sits evenly in the frame.
  • Wheels and rollers: wear, damage and movement condition.
  • Track: obstruction, wear or damage affecting travel.
  • Alignment: whether the shutter reaches the correct closing position.
  • Handle and lock: whether hardware engages correctly.
  • Rubber beading and gaps: whether sealing or deteriorated beading contributes to the problem.

Checking the complete operating system helps avoid treating the visible symptom while the actual cause remains.

Window Not Closing Repair Process

  1. Identify whether the window is stuck, hard to slide, reaching the frame unevenly or failing to lock.
  2. Inspect the existing window and shutter position.
  3. Check rollers, wheels and track condition where applicable.
  4. Check alignment and handle/lock engagement.
  5. Check visible gaps and rubber beading where relevant.
  6. Carry out suitable adjustment, repair or compatible component replacement and test closing and locking.

Window Not Closing Repair FAQs

Why is my sliding window not closing?

Possible causes include worn rollers or wheels, track problems, alignment, handles or locks.

Can rollers cause a window not to close?

Yes. Worn or damaged rollers can affect shutter position and movement.

Can alignment cause a window not to close?

Yes. If the shutter is not correctly positioned, it may not reach the proper closed or locked position.

Why does my window close but not lock?

Handle, lock, alignment or shutter-position problems can affect hardware engagement.

Does a window that will not close need replacement?

Not automatically. The existing window can first be assessed for repair, adjustment or compatible component replacement.
