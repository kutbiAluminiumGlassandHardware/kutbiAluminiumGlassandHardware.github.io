Loose or Broken Window Glass: Repair-First Assessment

When window glass is loose, cracked or broken, Kutbi checks the surrounding system before recommending the next step. Depending on the condition, the work may involve suitable glass replacement, checking rubber beading, assessing the frame and checking whether alignment or closing problems are contributing to movement or gaps.

  1. Inspect the glass condition and visible movement.
  2. Check how the glass is seated within the existing frame.
  3. Check visible rubber beading and sealing conditions.
  4. Assess the aluminium or uPVC frame and related window condition.
  5. Identify whether gaps, alignment or closing problems are also present.
  6. Recommend suitable repair or compatible glass replacement and test the completed window.

Loose Window Glass Repair FAQs

Why is my window glass loose?

Possible causes include deteriorated beading, changes in the glass seating or related frame and sealing conditions.

Can loose window glass be repaired?

The correct solution depends on the glass and frame condition. The existing system can be assessed before deciding on suitable repair or replacement.

Can broken glass be replaced in an aluminium window?

Suitable broken or cracked glass can be assessed for replacement within the existing aluminium frame where appropriate.

Can broken glass be replaced in a uPVC window?

Suitable uPVC window glass can be assessed for compatible replacement along with the surrounding beading and frame condition.

Does loose glass mean I need a new window?

Not automatically. The glass, beading and existing frame can first be assessed to determine whether suitable repair or component replacement is possible.

Can I send a photo first?

Yes. A clear photo of the full window and close-up of the loose, cracked or broken glass can help with an initial assessment.
