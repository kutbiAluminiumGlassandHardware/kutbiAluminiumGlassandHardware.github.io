Track + wheel + alignment diagnosis

Handle or Lock Problem? We Check the Sliding System Too

A sliding window or door may appear to have a handle or lock fault when worn wheels, rollers, track condition or alignment prevent the shutter from reaching the correct closing position. Kutbi checks these related components where applicable.

Track & Alignment Check

Handle & Lock Check

The hardware, fixing points and locking engagement are inspected for wear, damage and compatibility.

Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Repairs Window & Door Handles and Locks

1. Understand the problem

Share a clear photo and explain whether the handle is broken, loose, difficult to operate or the lock is not engaging.

2. Inspect the existing system

We check the aluminium or uPVC frame, handle, lock, closing position and related sliding hardware as applicable.

3. Diagnose the cause

We determine whether the issue is the handle, lock, alignment, wheel, roller, track or another related component.

4. Repair or replace

Where suitable, the affected hardware is repaired, adjusted or replaced with a compatible part.

5. Test operation

The window or door is tested for smooth movement, correct closing and proper locking after the work.
