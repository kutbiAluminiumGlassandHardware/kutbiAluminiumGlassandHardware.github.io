Repair-First Diagnosis

  1. Understand the symptom: confirm whether the door is hard to move, stuck, noisy or failing to close.
  2. Inspect the sliding system: check wheels or rollers, track, alignment and relevant hardware.
  3. Identify the actual fault: determine whether cleaning, adjustment, repair or compatible part replacement is appropriate.
  4. Carry out suitable work: repair or replace compatible components where possible.
  5. Test the door: check smooth movement, closing and locking after the work.

The exact solution depends on the existing aluminium profile, hardware, opening and installation condition.
