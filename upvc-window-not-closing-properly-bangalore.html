uPVC Window Not Closing Properly

If a uPVC window is not closing properly, Kutbi checks alignment, rollers, handles, locks and sealing before suitable repair.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

A closing problem can have more than one cause. Kutbi checks the existing system and related components before recommending suitable repair or compatible replacement.

  1. Inspect the reported closing problem.
  2. Check alignment, rollers and moving parts.
  3. Check handle, lock and sealing conditions.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement.
  5. Test closing and locking after the work.
