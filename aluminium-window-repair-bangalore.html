Track + Wheel + Alignment Diagnosis

A sliding window or door should not be repaired by replacing one component without checking the rest of the mechanism. Kutbi checks:

  • Wheels and rollers: wear, damage and smooth movement.
  • Track: obstruction, wear, damage and sliding condition.
  • Alignment: shutter position and even movement.
  • Hardware: handles, locks and compatible related components.
  • Beading: worn rubber beading when it contributes to the repair requirement.

Repair Existing Aluminium Windows Before Considering Replacement

If an existing aluminium window is stuck, hard to slide, noisy, dragging or not closing properly, the problem may be limited to wheels, rollers, the track, alignment or hardware. Kutbi first checks the existing window and identifies the actual fault before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement.

Repairing the affected component can often address the operating problem without replacing the complete window. Where a repair is not suitable, the condition of the existing system can be assessed so you can understand the next step.

Aluminium Sliding Window Repair Process

  1. Understand the symptom: stuck, heavy, noisy, dragging, misaligned or difficult to lock.
  2. Inspect the aluminium frame, shutter, wheels, rollers, track and alignment.
  3. Check relevant handles, locks and rubber beading.
  4. Identify the likely cause rather than replacing parts unnecessarily.
  5. Repair or replace compatible worn components where suitable.
  6. Adjust the sliding mechanism and alignment.
  7. Test opening, closing, sliding and locking before completion.

Signs Your Aluminium Window May Need Repair

  • The sliding shutter is hard to move, stuck or scraping.
  • The window does not close or lock correctly.
  • The shutter sits unevenly or leaves a visible gap.
  • Rollers or wheels appear worn or movement has become heavy.
  • The track is damaged, obstructed or worn.
  • Handle or lock operation has become difficult.
  • Rubber beading or sealing has deteriorated.
  • Window glass is cracked, shattered or loose.

Several symptoms can have the same visible result. Kutbi checks the related components so the repair targets the actual operating problem.

Aluminium Window Repair Process

  1. Identify the window problem: sliding, closing, locking, sealing, alignment or glass.
  2. Inspect the existing aluminium frame, shutter and relevant hardware.
  3. Check rollers, wheels and tracks on sliding windows.
  4. Check alignment, handles, locks, rubber beading and visible gaps where relevant.
  5. Assess damaged glass and determine whether suitable glass replacement can be carried out within the existing frame.
  6. Carry out the appropriate repair or compatible component replacement and test operation.
