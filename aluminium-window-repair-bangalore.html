Common Aluminium Window Problems We Repair

Is your aluminium sliding window hard to move, getting stuck halfway, scraping the track, making noise, wobbling, leaving a gap, or refusing to close and lock? These problems can often be related to worn wheels or rollers, track condition, alignment, handles, locks or rubber beading.

Aluminium Window Repair Process

  1. Inspect the shutter, frame, wheels or rollers and track.
  2. Identify the cause of sticking, noise, poor alignment or locking difficulty.
  3. Recommend suitable repair or hardware replacement.
  4. Complete the repair and adjust the shutter for smoother operation.
  5. Check sliding, closing and locking before completion.
