Problems We Repair on Existing Aluminium Windows

If an aluminium sliding shutter is hard to move, gets stuck halfway, scrapes the track, makes noise, wobbles, leaves a gap, or will not close and lock correctly, the cause may be worn wheels or rollers, track condition, alignment, a handle or lock, or deteriorated rubber beading.

Aluminium Window Repair Process

  1. Inspect the shutter, frame, wheels or rollers and track.
  2. Identify the cause of sticking, noise, poor alignment or locking difficulty.
  3. Recommend suitable repair or compatible hardware replacement.
  4. Complete the repair and adjust the shutter for smoother operation.
  5. Check sliding, closing and locking before completion.
