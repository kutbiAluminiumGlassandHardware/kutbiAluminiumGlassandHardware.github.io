Track, Wheel & Alignment Diagnosis

A sliding window or door may appear to have a track problem when the actual cause is a worn wheel or roller, poor alignment or another hardware issue. Kutbi checks the aluminium or uPVC shutter, frame, wheels, rollers, track, alignment and locking hardware to identify the likely cause before carrying out suitable work.

Track Condition

Check for worn, damaged, obstructed or uneven sliding paths.

Wheels & Rollers

Check worn or damaged rollers and wheels that can make the shutter drag or jam.

Alignment

Check shutter and frame alignment when movement is uneven or difficult.

Hardware

Check handles, locks, rubber beading and related compatible components.

Common Problems We Repair

If an aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door is hard to move, gets stuck halfway, scrapes the track, makes noise, wobbles, leaves a gap, or will not close and lock correctly, the cause may be worn wheels or rollers, track condition, alignment, a handle or lock, or deteriorated rubber beading.

Detailed Repair Process

  1. Inspect the aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door and understand the operating problem.
  2. Check the frame, shutter, wheels, rollers, track and alignment.
  3. Inspect handles, locks, rubber beading and other related hardware where relevant.
  4. Identify the likely cause of sticking, noise, poor movement, alignment or locking difficulty.
  5. Recommend suitable repair or compatible hardware replacement.
  6. Complete the repair and adjust the sliding system for smoother operation.
  7. Test opening, closing, sliding and locking before completion.
