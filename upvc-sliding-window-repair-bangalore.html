How Kutbi Diagnoses a uPVC Sliding Window Problem

  1. Check whether the sash is stuck, hard to slide or failing to close.
  2. Inspect rollers or wheels for wear, damage and uneven movement.
  3. Check the sliding track for obstruction, wear or damage.
  4. Check sash and frame alignment and whether the window sits correctly.
  5. Test handle and lock engagement after alignment checks.
  6. Check rubber beading, gaps and suitable glass condition where relevant.
  7. Repair or replace the affected component where practical and test the completed window.

This repair-first process helps identify the actual cause instead of automatically replacing the complete uPVC sliding window.
