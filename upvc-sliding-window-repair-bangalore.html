uPVC Sliding Window Track, Wheel & Alignment Diagnosis

1. Understand the symptom

We identify whether the window is stuck, noisy, dragging, difficult to slide, not closing or not locking.

2. Inspect the sliding system

We check accessible wheels, rollers, track condition, shutter alignment and related hardware to locate the likely cause.

3. Repair the actual fault

Where suitable, we service, adjust, repair or replace the affected compatible component instead of assuming the complete window needs replacement.

4. Test movement and locking

After the work, we check sliding movement, alignment, closing and locking to confirm the repaired system operates correctly.
