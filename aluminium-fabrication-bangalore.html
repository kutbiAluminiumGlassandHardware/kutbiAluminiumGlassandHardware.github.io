Aluminium Window & Door Installation

Glass & Hardware Compatibility

Fabrication is not only about the aluminium frame. Suitable glass, wheels or rollers, tracks, handles, locks, rubber beading and other compatible components may be required depending on the system.

For broken glass in an existing suitable frame, see our broken glass replacement service.

Our Aluminium Fabrication & Installation Process

  1. Understand the window or door requirement.
  2. Check the opening and take relevant measurements.
  3. Confirm the suitable frame and operating system.
  4. Plan compatible glass and hardware requirements.
  5. Fabricate the required aluminium frame components.
  6. Install and check frame positioning and operation.
  7. Make necessary alignment or hardware adjustments.

Repair, Replacement or New Fabrication?

Not every damaged aluminium window or door needs a new frame. If the existing system is serviceable, repair or component replacement may be more appropriate. If the frame or system is unsuitable for the requirement, new fabrication may be considered.

Our repair-first approach helps identify the actual problem before recommending unnecessary replacement.
