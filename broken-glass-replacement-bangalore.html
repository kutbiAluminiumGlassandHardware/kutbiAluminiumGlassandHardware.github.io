Detailed replacement process

How Kutbi Handles Broken Glass Replacement

  1. Understand the damage: Review the broken or cracked pane and the affected aluminium or uPVC window or door.
  2. Inspect the existing system: Check the frame, opening, glass seating and access requirements.
  3. Measure and confirm: Confirm dimensions and suitable replacement requirements.
  4. Remove damaged glass: Remove the damaged pane carefully according to the existing frame system.
  5. Install suitable replacement: Fit the confirmed replacement glass into the existing suitable frame.
  6. Check the finished opening: Inspect the glass seating and window or door operation after replacement.

Aluminium vs uPVC Glass Replacement

Aluminium windows and doors: Existing aluminium frames are inspected for glass seating, opening dimensions and frame condition before replacement.

uPVC windows and doors: Existing uPVC profiles and glass-retaining arrangements are checked because compatibility depends on the installed system.

Where the existing frame is suitable, replacing only the damaged glass can be a practical alternative to replacing the complete window or door.
