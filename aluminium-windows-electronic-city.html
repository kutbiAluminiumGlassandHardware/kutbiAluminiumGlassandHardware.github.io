Window repair services in Electronic City

Frequent opening and closing can wear sliding window rollers, wheels, locks and handles. Dust and age can also affect tracks and rubber beading. Kutbi checks the existing window and recommends a practical repair based on the component and condition.

  • Aluminium sliding window repair
  • Window roller replacement
  • Window wheel replacement
  • Aluminium window lock replacement
  • Window handle replacement
  • Rubber beading replacement
  • Track and alignment checks
  • Aluminium sliding door hardware repair

Repair or replacement?

A complete new window is not always necessary. If the aluminium frame and glass are usable, replacing worn hardware can restore operation. If the frame is damaged or unsuitable, Kutbi can assess whether replacement is more appropriate.
