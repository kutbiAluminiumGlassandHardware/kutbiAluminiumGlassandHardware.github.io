Common Causes of a Hard-to-Slide uPVC Window

A uPVC sliding window can become difficult to move because of worn rollers, damaged wheels, track problems, alignment issues or faulty hardware. Kutbi checks the complete sliding mechanism before recommending the suitable repair.

Worn or damaged rollers and wheels

Worn rollers or wheels can make the shutter heavy, noisy or difficult to move.

Track wear or obstruction

Dirt, obstruction, wear or damage in the track can affect smooth sliding movement.

Alignment problems

An uneven or misaligned shutter can drag against the frame or track.

Handle or lock problems

Faulty hardware can make operation and closing difficult.

Other worn hardware

Compatible hardware may need repair or replacement after the existing system is inspected.

Window not closing properly

Movement, alignment, wheel, track or hardware issues can prevent proper closing.
