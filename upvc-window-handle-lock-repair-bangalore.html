FAQs

Can a loose uPVC window handle be repaired?

Depending on the condition, the handle and fixing points can be checked for adjustment or compatible replacement.

Why is my uPVC window lock difficult to operate?

Alignment, handle operation, lock hardware or fixing issues can affect locking. The complete mechanism should be checked.

Do I need to replace the whole uPVC window?

Not necessarily. Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and checks the existing hardware and window before recommending larger replacement work.
