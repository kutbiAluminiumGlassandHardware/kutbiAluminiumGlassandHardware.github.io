Door lock problems

Aluminium Door Lock Problems

A faulty lock can make an aluminium door difficult to secure or operate. We inspect the lock together with the handle, alignment and related hardware before recommending a suitable repair.

Door Not Locking

If the lock does not engage correctly, the lock, latch position, handle and door alignment should be checked.

Door Not Unlocking

A lock that does not release normally may require inspection of the lock mechanism, handle connection or alignment.

Lock Not Latching

Incorrect latch engagement can be related to alignment, hardware position or the lock mechanism itself.

Stiff or Difficult Lock

Stiff operation can have several causes, so we check the lock and connected door hardware before replacing parts.

Hardware inspection

Handle & Lock Check

The handle and lock work together on many aluminium door systems. We inspect their connection and operation where relevant.

Door Alignment Check

Door position and alignment can affect whether the lock or latch reaches its correct position. We check alignment when the symptoms indicate it may be involved.

Lock Fixing Check

Mounting points and related hardware are checked to determine whether the existing lock can be secured, adjusted or repaired.

Compatible Lock Replacement

When a lock is not suitable for repair, we check for a compatible replacement for the existing aluminium door system rather than assuming every lock will fit.

Repair-first approach

Aluminium Door Lock Repair Process

  1. Understand the locking or unlocking problem.
  2. Inspect the lock, handle, latch and relevant hardware.
  3. Check door alignment and operation where necessary.
  4. Carry out suitable adjustment or repair.
  5. Use compatible replacement hardware when repair is not suitable.
  6. Test locking, unlocking and door operation after the work.
Troubleshooting

Common Aluminium Door Lock Problems

Common symptoms include a door that will not lock, will not unlock, does not latch correctly, or has a stiff or difficult lock. The same symptom can have different causes, so inspection is important.

Repair vs Replacement

A lock problem does not automatically mean the complete door needs replacement. Where suitable, we first look at adjustment, fixing or compatible lock hardware replacement.

Frequently asked questions

FAQs

Why is my aluminium door not locking?

Possible causes include lock or latch problems, handle issues or door alignment. Inspection helps identify the cause.

Can an aluminium door lock be repaired?

In suitable cases, yes. The lock and connected hardware can be inspected before deciding whether adjustment, repair or compatible replacement is appropriate.

Does a lock problem require a new door?

Not necessarily. A suitable lock or hardware repair may resolve the problem without replacing the complete door.
