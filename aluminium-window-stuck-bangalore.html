Aluminium Window Stuck Repair

For an aluminium window stuck or difficult to open, Kutbi diagnoses the sliding mechanism and related hardware before repair.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the existing track, wheels, rollers, alignment, handle and lock to identify the cause rather than treating only the symptom.

  1. Inspect the window and reported problem.
  2. Check track, wheels, rollers and alignment.
  3. Check handle, lock and related hardware.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement.
  5. Test movement and closing after the work.
