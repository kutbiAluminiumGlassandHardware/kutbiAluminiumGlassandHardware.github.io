Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Window & Door Track Repair

Tracks guide a sliding window or door along its opening. Dust, repeated use, worn wheels or rollers, damaged track sections and alignment problems can cause dragging, sticking, scraping or difficult operation. Kutbi checks the complete aluminium or uPVC sliding system before carrying out suitable repair work.

Aluminium Sliding Window & Door Track Problems

  • Bent, worn or damaged aluminium track
  • Worn sliding channel
  • Dirt or debris affecting movement
  • Aluminium window or door wheel and roller wear
  • Sliding shutter or door panel misalignment
  • Repeated sticking while opening or closing
  • Scraping or grinding during movement

uPVC Sliding Window & Door Track Problems

  • Worn or obstructed sliding track
  • Worn wheels or rollers
  • Panel or sash alignment problems
  • Dragging or difficult movement
  • Difficulty closing or locking after alignment changes
  • Unusual rubbing or scraping during operation

When the Track Is Not the Only Problem

A track may look like the problem when worn wheels, rollers, alignment, handle or lock position is actually preventing the shutter or door from reaching the correct position. Checking the whole sliding system helps avoid unnecessary replacement and makes the repair more targeted.

Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Handles Sliding Track & Alignment Repairs

1. Understand the symptom

We identify whether the window or door is stuck, dragging, noisy, scraping, difficult to close or difficult to lock.

2. Inspect the sliding system

We check the track, wheels, rollers, frame, shutter or door panel and alignment.

3. Diagnose the actual cause

We determine whether the issue is the track, alignment, wheel, roller or another related component.

4. Repair or replace where suitable

The affected component is repaired, adjusted or replaced according to the condition of the existing system.

5. Align and test

The sliding window or door is adjusted and tested for opening, closing and locking operation.

Related Repair Components

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my aluminium or uPVC sliding window difficult to move?

Worn wheels or rollers, damaged tracks, dirt and alignment problems can all cause difficult sliding. The complete mechanism should be inspected.

Can you repair an aluminium or uPVC sliding door track?

Yes. Suitable track, alignment and sliding-mechanism repairs can be carried out after inspecting the existing aluminium or uPVC sliding door system.

Do you also replace window and door wheels?

Yes. If worn wheels or rollers are contributing to the problem, suitable replacement hardware can be fitted.

Can you check alignment when a window or door will not close properly?

Yes. Kutbi checks panel position, track condition, wheels, rollers and alignment when difficult closing or locking may be related to the sliding system.

Can I send photos before arranging service?

Yes. You can share photos of the sliding window or door, track and problem area on WhatsApp for an initial assessment.
