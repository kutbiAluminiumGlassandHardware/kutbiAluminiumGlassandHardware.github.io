Aluminium Sliding Window Track Repair

The track is the path along which a sliding window moves. Dust, repeated use, damaged sections, worn rollers and alignment problems can make the shutter drag or become difficult to operate. Kutbi inspects the complete sliding mechanism before carrying out suitable repair work.

Track Problems That Can Affect Sliding

  • Bent or damaged aluminium track
  • Worn sliding channel
  • Dirt or debris affecting movement
  • Roller and wheel wear
  • Window shutter misalignment
  • Repeated sticking while opening or closing
  • Scraping or grinding during movement

Our Track Repair Process

  1. Inspect the window shutter and track.
  2. Check rollers, wheels and alignment.
  3. Identify the cause of difficult movement.
  4. Repair or replace suitable damaged components.
  5. Adjust the window and test smooth operation.

Why Check the Rollers Too?

A window can feel like it has a track problem when the actual cause is worn rollers or wheels. Checking both parts helps avoid unnecessary replacement and ensures the repair addresses the operating problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my aluminium sliding window difficult to move?

Worn rollers, damaged tracks, dirt and poor alignment can all cause difficult sliding. The complete mechanism should be inspected.

Can you repair a damaged window track?

Suitable track and sliding-mechanism repairs can be carried out after inspecting the existing aluminium window system.

Do you also replace window wheels?

Yes. If worn wheels or rollers are contributing to the problem, suitable replacement hardware can be fitted.

Can I send photos before arranging service?

Yes. You can share photos of the window and track on WhatsApp for an initial assessment.
