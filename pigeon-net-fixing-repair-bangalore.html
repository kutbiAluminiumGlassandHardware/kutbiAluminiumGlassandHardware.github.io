Loose fixing points

Loose Pigeon Net Fixing Points

Loose fixing points can allow sections of a bird net to sag, open gaps or move away from the intended coverage. We inspect the existing installation and repair suitable fixing points where the net and supporting areas can be serviced.

Anchors & hooks

Anchors, Hooks & Fixing Hardware

We check accessible hooks, anchors and fixing areas for looseness, movement or visible damage. Where suitable, the affected fixing can be adjusted, re-fixed or serviced without automatically replacing the complete net installation.

Net re-fixing

Pigeon Net Re-fixing Repair

If an existing net has come away from suitable fixing points, we assess the affected section and re-fix it where practical. The repair is focused on restoring secure coverage rather than unnecessary full replacement.

Damaged fixing areas

Damaged or Failed Fixing Points

We inspect the fixing location, surrounding installation area and net condition. If the fixing issue is limited and the existing net remains suitable, a targeted repair may be possible. If the net itself is worn or damaged beyond practical repair, replacement can be discussed.

Balcony & openings

Balcony and Window Opening Fixing Repair

Fixing repairs can be relevant to apartment balconies, service openings and other suitable bird-net installations. We assess the accessible fixing line and the condition of the existing installation before recommending the repair needed.

High-rise checks

High-Rise Pigeon Net Fixing Inspection

For higher-level balconies and openings, the actual access and installation condition must be assessed before work. We focus on suitable, accessible repair requirements and do not assume that every installation can be repaired in the same way.

Repair-first approach

Fixing Repair vs Pigeon Net Replacement

A loose fixing does not automatically mean the whole net needs replacement. We first check whether the existing net and fixing system are suitable for repair. If the net is worn, torn or unsuitable for continued service, replacement may be the more appropriate option.

FAQs

Pigeon Net Fixing Repair FAQs

Can a loose pigeon net be repaired?

Yes, where the existing net and fixing system are suitable, a loose section may be re-fixed or repaired.

Do you replace the full net for a loose fixing?

Not automatically. We first assess whether a targeted fixing repair is practical.

What if the net itself is damaged?

We assess the extent of damage and can discuss repair or replacement based on the condition of the existing installation.
