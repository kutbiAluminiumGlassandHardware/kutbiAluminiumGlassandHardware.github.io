Repair Process

  1. Assess the existing door and how it came off track.
  2. Inspect accessible wheels, rollers, track and alignment.
  3. Identify damaged or worn components.
  4. Carry out suitable restoration, adjustment or compatible replacement.
  5. Test sliding, closing and locking.
