Why a Window May Not Close Properly

A window that will not close properly can have a problem with the rollers or wheels, track, alignment, handle, lock or rubber beading. Kutbi inspects the existing system before recommending the suitable repair.

For sliding windows, the shutter may be sitting incorrectly because of worn wheels, rollers or track conditions. For other aluminium and uPVC systems, hardware or alignment can affect closing and locking.
