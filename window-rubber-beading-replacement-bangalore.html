Signs Your Window or Door Beading May Need Replacement

  • Rubber has become hard, brittle or cracked
  • Beading is loose, displaced or coming out
  • Visible gaps around the glass
  • Water entering around the glass during rain
  • Dust or air gaps around the frame
  • Glass rattling because the seal has deteriorated
  • Old beading no longer sits securely
  • Sealing around an aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door has deteriorated

Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Replaces Window & Door Rubber Beading

1. Understand the problem

We review the reported leak, gap, loose beading, rattling glass or sealing issue and inspect photos where available.

2. Inspect the system

The aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door, frame, glass seating and existing beading are checked.

3. Diagnose the cause

We determine whether beading is the main issue or whether frame fit, alignment or another component also needs attention.

4. Remove damaged beading

Where suitable, deteriorated beading is removed and the seating area is cleaned and prepared.

5. Fit compatible replacement

Suitable replacement beading is fitted according to the existing profile and glass arrangement.

6. Check final fit and sealing

The glass position, beading fit and sealing are checked, followed by an operating check where the system is a sliding window or door.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does rubber beading become damaged?

Rubber can deteriorate over time because of age, repeated use and exposure to sunlight and weather.

Can you replace beading in aluminium sliding windows and doors?

Yes. Kutbi provides suitable aluminium sliding window and door rubber beading replacement after checking the existing system.

Can you replace beading in uPVC sliding windows and doors?

Yes. Suitable uPVC sliding window and door beading can be replaced depending on the frame and profile.

Can rubber beading be replaced without replacing the whole window or door?

In suitable window and door systems, worn beading can often be replaced without replacing the complete frame.

Can beading problems cause water or air gaps?

Damaged, hardened or displaced beading can contribute to poor sealing, although the frame and glass seating should also be checked.
