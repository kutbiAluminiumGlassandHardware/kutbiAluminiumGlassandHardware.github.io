Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Replaces Window & Door Rubber Beading

1. Understand the problem

We review the reported leak, gap, loose beading, rattling glass or sealing issue and inspect photos where available.

2. Inspect the system

The aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door, frame, glass seating and existing beading are checked.

3. Diagnose the cause

We determine whether beading is the main issue or whether frame fit, alignment or another component also needs attention.

4. Remove damaged beading

Where suitable, deteriorated beading is removed and the seating area is cleaned and prepared.

5. Fit compatible replacement

Suitable replacement beading is fitted according to the existing profile and glass arrangement.

6. Check final fit and sealing

The glass position, beading fit and sealing are checked, followed by an operating check where the system is a sliding window or door.

Signs Your Window Rubber Beading May Need Replacement

  • Rubber has become hard, brittle, loose or visibly damaged.
  • There is a visible gap around the glass or opening.
  • Air enters around the window even when it is closed.
  • Water leakage occurs around an area with deteriorated sealing.
  • The seal no longer sits correctly around the existing window system.
  • Beading is displaced or no longer provides a consistent seal.

These symptoms can overlap with alignment or closing problems, so Kutbi checks the related window condition instead of treating every gap as a beading-only issue.

Window Rubber Beading Replacement Process

  1. Identify where the seal or beading problem is visible.
  2. Inspect the existing rubber, glass/frame area and surrounding sealing condition.
  3. Check for related gaps, alignment or closing issues.
  4. Assess whether suitable beading replacement can address the affected area.
  5. Carry out suitable beading/seal replacement on the existing system where appropriate.
  6. Check the finished seal and window condition after the work.
