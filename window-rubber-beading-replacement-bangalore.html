Window Rubber Beading Replacement in Bangalore

Rubber beading helps hold and seal glass within suitable window and door frames. With age and regular exposure to weather, beading may become hard, cracked, loose or damaged. Replacing worn beading can improve the fit and sealing of the window without replacing the entire frame.

Signs Your Window Beading May Need Replacement

  • Visible cracks or hardened rubber
  • Loose or displaced beading
  • Water entering around the glass during rain
  • Dust or air gaps around the frame
  • Glass rattling because the seal has deteriorated
  • Old rubber that no longer sits securely

Our Beading Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the frame, glass and existing beading.
  2. Remove damaged or deteriorated beading where suitable.
  3. Clean the frame and seating area.
  4. Fit compatible replacement beading.
  5. Check the glass position and final sealing.

Aluminium and uPVC Window Beading

Kutbi provides beading replacement for suitable aluminium and uPVC window and door systems. The correct profile depends on the existing frame and glass arrangement, so the system should be checked before replacement.

Why does window rubber beading become damaged?

Rubber can deteriorate over time because of age, repeated use and exposure to sunlight and weather.

In suitable window systems, worn beading can often be replaced without replacing the complete frame.
