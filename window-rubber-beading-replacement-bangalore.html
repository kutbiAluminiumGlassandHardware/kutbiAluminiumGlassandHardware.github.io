Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Window and Door Beading Replacement

Rubber beading helps hold and seal glass within suitable aluminium and uPVC window and door frames. With age and regular exposure to sunlight and weather, beading can become hard, cracked, loose or damaged. Replacing suitable worn beading can improve the fit and sealing of the glass without replacing the entire frame.

Common Problems We Diagnose

  • Cracked, hardened or brittle rubber beading
  • Loose or displaced beading
  • Water entering around the glass during rain
  • Dust or air gaps around the frame
  • Glass movement or rattling because the seal has deteriorated
  • Old beading that no longer sits securely
  • Gaps or poor sealing around aluminium or uPVC sliding windows and doors

Our Detailed Beading Replacement Process

  1. Inspect the aluminium or uPVC sliding window or door.
  2. Check the frame, glass seating and existing rubber beading.
  3. Diagnose whether the problem is caused by beading, frame fit, alignment or another component.
  4. Remove damaged or deteriorated beading where suitable.
  5. Clean and prepare the seating area.
  6. Fit compatible replacement beading for the existing profile.
  7. Check glass position, sealing and final fit.
  8. Test the window or door operation where the beading is part of a sliding system.

Aluminium Sliding Windows & Doors

Aluminium frames can use different beading profiles depending on the frame and glass arrangement. Kutbi checks the existing aluminium sliding window or door before recommending suitable replacement beading.

uPVC Sliding Windows & Doors

uPVC systems can also use profile-specific sealing and beading. Kutbi checks the existing uPVC window or door profile and glass arrangement so the replacement is suitable for the system.

Beading vs. Other Window Problems

Not every leak, rattle or difficult sliding problem is caused by rubber beading. The frame, glass seating, alignment, wheels, rollers and tracks may also need inspection. A proper diagnosis helps identify the actual repair requirement.
