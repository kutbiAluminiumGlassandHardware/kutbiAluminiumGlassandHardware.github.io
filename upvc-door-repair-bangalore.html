Repair process

How Kutbi Handles a uPVC Sliding Door Repair

  1. Inspect: Check the door, wheels, rollers, track, alignment and hardware.
  2. Identify: Determine whether the problem is related to worn wheels, rollers, track condition, alignment, lock, handle or another compatible component.
  3. Repair or replace: Carry out suitable adjustment, servicing or compatible part replacement based on the existing door.
  4. Test: Check sliding movement, closing, alignment and locking operation after the work.

Repair requirements vary by uPVC profile, door design and existing hardware, so final compatibility and scope are confirmed from the actual door.
