Detailed repair process

How Kutbi Handles a uPVC Sliding Door Repair

  1. Understand the complaint: Identify whether the door is stuck, noisy, hard to slide, rubbing, not closing or not locking.
  2. Inspect the existing system: Check wheels, rollers, track, alignment, handle, lock, beading and related hardware as applicable.
  3. Find the actual cause: Separate wheel/roller problems from track, alignment or hardware issues.
  4. Repair or replace: Carry out suitable cleaning, adjustment, repair or compatible component replacement.
  5. Test the door: Check sliding movement, alignment, closing and locking after the work.

Compatibility depends on the existing uPVC profile, door design and hardware, so the final scope is confirmed from the actual door.
