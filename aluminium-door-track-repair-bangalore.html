Aluminium Door Track Repair

This page covers aluminium door track repair for existing sliding doors. Kutbi checks the track together with wheels, rollers and alignment before recommending suitable repair.

Common Problems We Check

Worn track

Repeated sliding can wear the track channel and affect smooth door movement.

Obstructed track

Dirt or debris can interfere with wheels and cause dragging or sticking.

Damaged track

Visible damage or uneven sections can affect sliding and closing.

Wheel or roller problem

Worn wheels can create symptoms that appear to be a track problem, so both are checked.

Alignment problem

Incorrect panel position can change how the door moves along the track.

Closing difficulty

Track and alignment issues can prevent the door from reaching its correct closing and locking position.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the existing system before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement. We look at the related parts that can cause the same symptom instead of assuming one component is always responsible.

  1. Inspect the reported problem and operating condition.
  2. Check the related hardware, alignment, sealing or sliding mechanism.
  3. Identify the likely cause from the existing system.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement where appropriate.
  5. Test operation, closing and locking after the work.
