Aluminium door solutions

Aluminium Sliding Doors

Kutbi supplies and services suitable aluminium sliding doors for residential and commercial requirements. We handle fabrication and installation for suitable new openings and repair existing doors when movement, hardware or closing problems develop.

Aluminium Door Fabrication

Custom aluminium door fabrication is available for suitable openings and requirements. Profile, opening dimensions, design and hardware requirements are considered before fabrication.

Aluminium Door Installation

We provide installation for suitable aluminium doors and sliding-door systems. The opening and operating requirements are checked so the installed door can be tested for movement and closing.

Existing door service

Aluminium Door Repair

For existing aluminium doors that are stuck, dragging, difficult to close, not locking or making unusual noise, Kutbi follows a repair-first approach. The door and relevant operating components are inspected before suitable adjustment, repair or compatible replacement is recommended.

Wheels & Rollers

Worn or damaged wheels and rollers can affect sliding movement. See Aluminium Door Roller Repair.

Tracks & Alignment

Track and alignment conditions can cause dragging or uneven movement. See Aluminium Door Alignment Repair and Sliding Door Repair.

Handles & Locks

Loose, broken or difficult hardware can affect operation. See Aluminium Door Handle Repair and Aluminium Door Lock Repair.

Door Glass

Suitable broken door glass can be replaced after checking the opening and requirement. See Door Glass Replacement.

Rubber Beading

Worn or deteriorated beading may need suitable replacement. See Rubber Beading Replacement.

FAQs

Do you install new aluminium doors?

Yes, suitable aluminium doors and sliding-door systems can be fabricated and installed according to the opening and requirement.

Do you repair existing aluminium sliding doors?

Yes. We inspect suitable existing doors for wheels, rollers, tracks, alignment, handles, locks and other operating problems.

Can you replace a damaged aluminium door lock or handle?

Suitable locks and handles can be repaired or replaced with compatible hardware after checking the existing system.
