Why a Door May Not Close Properly

Alignment problem

Panel position or frame alignment can affect closing, latching and smooth movement.

Worn wheels or rollers

Sliding doors may drag or sit unevenly when compatible wheels or rollers are worn.

Track problem

Obstruction, wear or damage in the sliding track can prevent the panel from reaching its correct position.

Handle or lock problem

A stiff, loose or damaged handle or lock can stop an otherwise aligned door from latching correctly.

Rubber beading or seal

Worn or displaced beading can affect fit, sealing and closing pressure.

Glass or frame condition

Where suitable, Kutbi checks the existing glass and frame condition when they contribute to the door problem.

Door Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the complete existing aluminium or uPVC door before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement. The aim is to identify the actual cause rather than replacing unrelated parts.

  1. Inspect the door movement and closing position.
  2. Check alignment, wheels, rollers and accessible tracks.
  3. Check handles, locks and latching points.
  4. Inspect visible rubber beading and sealing conditions.
  5. Assess the frame and suitable glass condition where relevant.
  6. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement and test the door.

Door Not Closing Properly: FAQs

Why is my sliding door not closing?

Common causes include worn wheels or rollers, track problems, alignment issues, handles or locks. Kutbi checks the complete sliding system.

Can an aluminium door that is not closing be repaired?

Where the existing system remains suitable, the cause can be diagnosed and repaired or a compatible component can be replaced.

Can a uPVC door that is not closing be repaired?

Yes, suitable uPVC doors can be checked for alignment, hardware, locking and sealing problems before replacement is considered.
