How Kutbi approaches the repair

Track + Wheel + Alignment Diagnosis

A sliding window or door should not be repaired by replacing one component without checking the rest of the mechanism. Kutbi checks the complete system where relevant:

  • Wheels and rollers: wear, damage and smooth movement.
  • Track: obstruction, wear, damage and sliding condition.
  • Alignment: shutter position and even movement.
  • Hardware: handles, locks and compatible related components.
  • Beading: worn rubber beading when it contributes to the repair requirement.

Common Aluminium & uPVC Window and Door Repair Problems

  • Window or door is stuck or difficult to operate.
  • Sliding window or door is hard to move.
  • Window or door does not close or lock properly.
  • Wheels or rollers are worn, noisy or damaged.
  • Track is worn, obstructed, damaged or affecting movement.
  • Handle or lock is loose, broken or not engaging correctly.
  • Rubber beading or sealing has deteriorated.
  • Air or water leakage occurs around the window or door.
  • Window glass is cracked, shattered or broken.

The same symptom can have different causes. Kutbi checks the related system before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement.

Repair First — Replace Only When Necessary

A damaged wheel, roller, track, handle, lock, beading or alignment issue does not automatically mean the complete window or door needs replacement. Kutbi first assesses the existing system and identifies whether a suitable repair or compatible component replacement can address the problem.

This repair-first approach is especially useful when an aluminium or uPVC window or door is still structurally suitable but has an operating, sealing, hardware, glass or sliding-system problem.
