How Kutbi approaches the repair

Track + Wheel + Alignment Diagnosis

A sliding window or door should not be repaired by replacing one component without checking the rest of the mechanism. Kutbi checks the complete system where relevant:

  • Wheels and rollers: wear, damage and smooth movement.
  • Track: obstruction, wear, damage and sliding condition.
  • Alignment: shutter position and even movement.
  • Hardware: handles, locks and compatible related components.
  • Beading: worn rubber beading when it contributes to the repair requirement.

Window & Door Repair FAQs

Do you repair both aluminium and uPVC windows and doors?

Yes. Kutbi handles suitable aluminium and uPVC window and door repair, including sliding windows and doors and common hardware and movement problems.

Do you repair uPVC sliding windows and doors?

Yes. See the dedicated uPVC sliding window and uPVC sliding door repair pages for those specific requirements.

Do you replace mosquito mesh?

Yes. Kutbi provides suitable mosquito mesh installation, repair and replacement for windows and doors, including Velcro, magnetic and pleated mesh options.

Can window wheels, rollers and tracks be repaired?

They can be inspected as part of the complete sliding mechanism. Suitable compatible wheels, rollers or track-related repairs are carried out where appropriate.

Wheel, roller, track, alignment, handle or lock problems can affect closing and locking. Kutbi checks the relevant parts to identify the repair requirement.
