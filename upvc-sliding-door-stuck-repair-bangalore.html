Common Causes of a Stuck uPVC Sliding Door

Roller problem

Worn or damaged rollers can make a sliding door heavy, uneven or jammed.

Track problem

How Kutbi Checks a Stuck Door

  1. Inspect the existing uPVC sliding door and frame.
  2. Check rollers, track, alignment and operating hardware.
  3. Identify the likely cause rather than replacing unrelated parts.
  4. Repair or replace a compatible component where suitable.
  5. Test movement and closing after the repair.
