Common questions

Before you book

Can every window be repaired?

Not every window is repairable. Suitability depends on the frame, profile, glass, hardware condition and availability of compatible replacement parts.

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Photos can help with an initial assessment, although exact diagnosis and parts may require an on-site inspection.

Do you replace the complete window?

Kutbi can also supply and install aluminium or uPVC windows where repair is not the appropriate solution.
