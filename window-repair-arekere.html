How Kutbi Approaches Repair

We check the existing frame, shutter, wheels or rollers, track, alignment and operating hardware before recommending the repair. A window that will not lock, for example, may also have a wheel, track or alignment problem preventing the shutter from reaching the correct position.

  1. Understand the problem and inspect the existing system.
  2. Identify the component or adjustment causing the operating issue.
  3. Repair or adjust the existing system where practical.
  4. Replace compatible worn parts where required and available.
  5. Test movement, closing and locking after the work.
