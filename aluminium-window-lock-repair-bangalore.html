Window Not Locking

If the handle moves but the window does not secure, the lock, handle, alignment and related hardware should be checked together. Kutbi identifies the relevant cause before recommending repair or compatible replacement.

Window Not Unlocking

A lock that will not release may be affected by the lock mechanism, handle operation, sash position or alignment. The system is inspected rather than assuming the lock alone is faulty.

Lock Not Latching

When the lock does not engage correctly, alignment, fixing points and compatible locking hardware can all be relevant. Kutbi checks these components before repair.

Stiff or Difficult Lock

Unusual resistance can come from the lock itself or from pressure caused by a misaligned sash. A suitable repair depends on the actual condition of the window.

Handle & Lock Check

Handles and locks work together. Kutbi checks both when the window is difficult to secure, difficult to open or requires excessive force during operation.

Alignment Check

A sash that sits unevenly or rubs against the frame can prevent a lock from engaging correctly. Alignment, rollers and track condition may therefore need inspection before a lock replacement is considered.

Lock Fixing & Hardware Check

Loose or damaged fixing points and worn compatible hardware can affect lock operation. Kutbi checks the existing installation and recommends the suitable repair where practical.

Compatible Lock Replacement

If a lock is damaged beyond practical repair, Kutbi can assess the existing aluminium window system and recommend a suitable compatible replacement rather than changing unrelated components.

Aluminium Window Lock Repair Process

  1. Understand whether the window will lock, unlock or latch.
  2. Inspect lock and handle operation.
  3. Check sash alignment, rollers and track where relevant.
  4. Inspect fixing points and compatible hardware.
  5. Determine whether adjustment, repair or replacement is suitable.
  6. Complete the required work and test locking and opening operation.

Common Aluminium Window Lock Problems

Lock does not engage

The lock may need adjustment or the sash may require alignment checking.

Lock feels loose

Fixing points and surrounding hardware should be inspected.

Lock is stiff

Lock condition and window alignment can both affect operation.

Lock is damaged

A compatible replacement can be assessed when practical repair is not possible.

Repair or Lock Replacement?

Not every lock problem requires replacement. Where adjustment or repair can restore suitable operation, that option can be considered. If the lock is damaged beyond practical repair, compatible replacement may be appropriate.

Kutbi follows a repair-first approach and checks the actual window system before recommending replacement.

FAQs

Why is my aluminium window not locking?

Possible causes include a faulty lock, handle issue, sash misalignment or related hardware problems.

Can an aluminium window lock be repaired?

Yes, suitable locks can be inspected for adjustment or repair before replacement is considered.

Can alignment affect window locking?

Yes. If the sash is not positioned correctly, the lock may not engage as intended.

Do I always need a new lock?

No. Kutbi checks whether the existing lock can be repaired or adjusted before recommending a compatible replacement.
