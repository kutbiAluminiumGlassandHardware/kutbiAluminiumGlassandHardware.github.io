Common Problems We Check

Water around door edges

Gaps around the frame or sealing can allow rainwater to enter.

Worn rubber beading

Hardened, loose or damaged beading can reduce sealing.

Alignment-related gaps

A door that does not sit correctly can create uneven sealing contact.

Glass and frame condition

Where relevant, the existing glass, frame and surrounding sealing are checked together.

Sliding door sealing

Suitable sliding doors are checked for panel position, movement and sealing conditions.

Drainage and surrounding conditions

The door system and visible surrounding conditions are assessed before recommending repair.

Repair by Problem: Diagnosis & Suitable Repair

Kutbi checks the existing system before recommending suitable repair or compatible component replacement. We look at the related parts that can cause the same symptom instead of assuming one component is always responsible.

  1. Inspect the reported problem and operating condition.
  2. Check the related hardware, alignment, sealing or sliding mechanism.
  3. Identify the likely cause from the existing system.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement where appropriate.
  5. Test operation, closing and locking after the work.
