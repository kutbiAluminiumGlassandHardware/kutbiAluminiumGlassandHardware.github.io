How Kutbi Diagnoses a Sliding Door

  1. Check the symptom: movement, dragging, closing, locking or hardware problem.
  2. Inspect wheels and rollers: determine whether compatible replacement is required.
  3. Check track and alignment: identify problems affecting smooth movement and closing.
  4. Inspect handles and locks: check related operating hardware.
  5. Repair or adjust: carry out suitable servicing or repair where possible.
  6. Test operation: check sliding, closing and locking after the work.

The exact repair depends on the aluminium or uPVC profile, existing hardware and installation condition.

Track-focused repair

Track & Alignment Problems

Sliding doors can drag, scrape, wobble or fail to close properly when the track and door alignment are affected. Kutbi checks the track together with the rollers and door position to identify the likely cause.

Track Inspection

We check for visible track wear, obstruction, damage and conditions that may affect roller movement. The repair depends on the existing door system and the condition of the track.

Track Repair vs Replacement

Where suitable, adjustment, cleaning or repair may be considered first. If the existing track cannot be suitably repaired, the appropriate replacement option depends on the profile and system.

FAQs

Can you repair an aluminium sliding door track?

Suitable track problems can be inspected and repaired where the existing system allows it. The track is checked together with the rollers and alignment.

Why is my sliding door hard to move?

Possible causes include worn rollers, track problems, obstruction or alignment issues. Inspection helps identify the actual cause.

Do I need to replace the complete door?

Not necessarily. A repair or compatible hardware replacement may resolve the problem depending on the condition of the existing system.
