Aluminium Door Roller Problems

We inspect rollers, wheels, tracks and alignment and carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement where practical.

Worn or Damaged Rollers

Worn wheels can make an aluminium sliding door heavy, rough or uneven. We check the roller condition before recommending a suitable repair or compatible replacement.

Door Hard to Slide

A stiff door may involve rollers, track condition, alignment or another hardware issue. We inspect the system rather than assuming the roller is the only cause.

Noise & Uneven Movement

Grinding, scraping, wobbling or uneven movement can indicate roller wear, track issues or alignment problems that need inspection.

Roller & Track Check

Rollers work with the door track. We check both areas and related hardware so the repair addresses the movement problem as accurately as practical.

Roller Replacement

When a roller is beyond practical repair, we can assess compatible replacement options for the existing aluminium sliding-door system.

Alignment Check

Incorrect door alignment can increase load on rollers and affect movement. Alignment is checked as part of the diagnosis where relevant.

Repair Process

We inspect the door movement, identify the likely hardware issue, check the roller and track, and then carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement where practical.

Common Problems

Stiff movement, noisy rollers, uneven sliding, worn wheels, track-related resistance and doors that become difficult to operate are common reasons for inspection.

Repair vs Replacement

Repair is considered where practical. If a roller is worn or damaged beyond useful repair, a compatible replacement may be more suitable after inspection.

FAQs

Why is my aluminium sliding door hard to move?

Roller wear, track condition, alignment or other hardware issues can cause resistance. Inspection helps identify the actual cause.

Can every door roller be repaired?

No. Repair depends on the roller and door system. Where repair is not practical, compatible replacement can be assessed.

Do you check the track too?

Yes. Roller and track interaction can affect door movement, so both are checked where relevant.
