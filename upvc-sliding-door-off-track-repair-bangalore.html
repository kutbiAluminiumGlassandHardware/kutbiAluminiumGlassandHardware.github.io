How Kutbi Diagnoses an Off-Track Door

  1. Inspect the panel, frame and operating condition.
  2. Check rollers, track and alignment.
  3. Identify the cause of the panel leaving its normal track position.
  4. Carry out suitable repair or compatible replacement.
  5. Test sliding, closing and locking.
