What Can Cause a uPVC Sliding Window to Become Hard to Move?

Common causes include worn wheels or rollers, track problems, poor alignment, damaged hardware and normal wear of the sliding mechanism. A window can also feel difficult to operate when the shutter is rubbing or the locking hardware is not aligned correctly.

Kutbi checks the actual system before recommending the repair. If the wheels are worn, wheel replacement may solve the movement problem; if the track or alignment is contributing, those issues are assessed at the same time.
