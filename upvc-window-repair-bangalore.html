Detailed repair process

uPVC Sliding Window & Door Repair Process

1. Understand the problem

Send a clear photo or explain whether the uPVC window or door is stuck, noisy, difficult to slide, not closing or not locking.

2. Track, wheel & alignment diagnosis

We inspect accessible wheels, rollers, tracks, alignment, handles, locks, beading and related hardware as applicable.

3. Repair or replace compatible parts

Where suitable, we clean, adjust, repair or replace the affected compatible component instead of recommending unnecessary full-system replacement.

4. Test movement and closing

The repaired system is checked for smoother movement, proper closing and locking, and correct operation after the work.
