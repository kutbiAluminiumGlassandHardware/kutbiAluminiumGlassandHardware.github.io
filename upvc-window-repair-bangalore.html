How we approach the repair

uPVC Window & Door Repair Process

1. Understand the problem

Send a photo or describe whether the uPVC window or door is stuck, jammed, noisy, difficult to slide, not closing or not locking.

2. Inspect the mechanism

We check accessible wheels, rollers, tracks, handles, locks, alignment and seals as applicable.

3. Repair or replace compatible parts

Where suitable, we repair, adjust or replace the affected hardware rather than recommending unnecessary full replacement.

4. Test the window or door

The window or door is checked for smoother movement, closing and locking after the work.
