Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my uPVC sliding window not sliding?

A uPVC sliding window may become difficult to move because of worn wheels or rollers, track problems or misalignment. Kutbi checks the sliding mechanism and repairs or replaces the affected component where suitable.

How do I fix a stuck uPVC sliding window?

A stuck uPVC sliding window should be checked for wheel, roller, track and alignment problems. Kutbi identifies the cause and carries out the appropriate repair.

Why is my uPVC window hard to open?

Worn rollers, wheel problems, track issues or alignment problems can make a uPVC window difficult to open. Kutbi checks the window to identify the required repair.

Can uPVC sliding window rollers be replaced?

Yes. Worn or damaged uPVC sliding window rollers can often be replaced with compatible hardware without replacing the complete window.

Can uPVC window wheels be replaced?

Yes. Damaged or worn uPVC window wheels can often be replaced with suitable compatible wheels after checking the existing window mechanism.

Can a uPVC sliding window track be repaired?

A uPVC sliding window track can be inspected for damage, wear and alignment problems. Depending on its condition, suitable repair or adjustment may be possible.

Why is my uPVC window not closing properly?

A uPVC window may not close properly because of wheel, roller, track, alignment or hardware problems. Kutbi checks the complete window mechanism to identify the cause.

Why is my uPVC window not locking?

A faulty handle or lock, incorrect alignment, or problems with the sliding components can prevent a uPVC window from locking properly. Kutbi checks the window and related hardware.

Can a uPVC window handle or lock be replaced?

Yes. Damaged or faulty uPVC window handles and locks can generally be replaced with suitable compatible hardware after checking the existing mechanism.

How much does uPVC window repair cost in Bangalore?
