Detailed repair process

uPVC Sliding Window & Door Track, Wheel & Alignment Diagnosis

1. Understand the symptom

Identify whether the window or door is stuck, noisy, difficult to slide, not closing or not locking.

2. Inspect the sliding system

Check accessible wheels, rollers, track condition, shutter alignment and relevant hardware.

3. Repair the actual fault

Clean, adjust, repair or replace the affected compatible component where suitable.

4. Test movement and closing

Check smooth movement, alignment, closing and locking after the work.

Aluminium & uPVC Sliding Windows and Doors

Sliding problems can affect both aluminium and uPVC sliding windows and doors. Wheels, rollers, tracks, alignment, handles, locks and rubber beading should be assessed according to the actual frame and hardware.
