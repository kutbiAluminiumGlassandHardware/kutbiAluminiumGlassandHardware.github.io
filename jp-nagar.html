Aluminium sliding window repair in JP Nagar

Kutbi Aluminium Glass & Hardware takes a repair-first approach. If an existing aluminium window can be restored safely and economically, we focus on the worn component instead of automatically recommending complete replacement. This is useful for apartments, independent homes, offices and shops where the existing frame is still serviceable.

Common symptoms include a panel that becomes heavy to slide, scraping or noisy movement, a loose handle, a lock that does not engage, damaged rubber beading or a window that is no longer aligned correctly. The exact repair depends on the frame and hardware already installed.
